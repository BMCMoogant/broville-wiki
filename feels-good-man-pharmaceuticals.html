Company and healthcare

Feels Good Man Pharmaceuticals, also abbreviated FGMP in community descriptions, is a Broville biotechnology and pharmacology company presenting itself as a wellness business. The community account places its headquarters in the Downtown business district and associates it with clinical care and medicines, including the HapsyEx™ antidepressants.

Research and products

The community account describes a specialization in synthetic chemicals and biological mutations, and connects the company historically to SOLAR, which it initially employed as a software subcontractor. It also attributes the following research to FGMP:

  • The Melon Project, a collaboration with SOLAR on mutant plants, notably Radon-Enriched Melons, intended as food sources or potential weaponry able to grow in extreme conditions.
  • Research into the regenerative properties of Nether Wart for exceptionally powerful healing potions, presented as a suggestion from clues rather than a demonstrated result.

Controversies and continuity

Community reports call the company “sketchy,” describe military research and ethically questionable human experimentation, and suspect it of using marginalized residents to test chemical compounds. These claims go beyond what the supplied records confirm about its clinic and products.

The same account suggests that FGMP remained active more discreetly after SOLAR's reported 2002 bankruptcy. It also alleges that a plutonium bomb exploded in SOLAR's stockpiles in 2016 and that nearby FGMP chemicals caused uncontrolled vegetation mutations within a 500-meter radius. This incident and the company's causal role are unconfirmed in the books supplied here, so they do not establish a dated disaster in the city's history.
