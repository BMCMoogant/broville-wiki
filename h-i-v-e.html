H.I.V.E.

OrganizationsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A research facility beneath the old city. Emergency signs connect its reactor to Broville’s disaster.

The complex beneath the old city

H.I.V.E. housed the Latency Reactor, called the Lag Generator by residents. In the Doctor’s second report, the machine extracted energy and matter from the world itself. The researcher credits it with changes in the old Broville’s commerce, industry, and entertainment.

The sinking

Two signs on the lower levels, at −174, 5, 1840 and −174, 5, 1841, mark the emergency area. One orders people to keep away from H.I.V.E.; the other warns of the reactor’s radioactive meltdown. In his first report, the Doctor attributes the sinking to the machine, accepts responsibility, and judges that the complex concentrated too much power in a confined space.

What was recovered

In the following weeks, the Doctor describes ruined streets, improvised shelters, and toxic ground. Construction teams removed the reactor from the epicenter, but investigation into the failure made little progress. Some residents moved to villages beside the lakes. The Doctor and the remaining scientists set up a laboratory in the forest to the west to study the machine. This laboratory was named SOLAR.

Continuity between versions

The revision of H.I.V.E.’s and SOLAR’s roles during map production is discussed in From v10 to v11. The first report establishes the sequence of the old complex, the disaster, and the machine’s removal, but leaves the year of the sinking and the mechanism of failure unresolved.
