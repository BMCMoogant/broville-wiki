Brian Garlick’s Expedition

EventsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A SOLAR team interprets desert remains as instructions for a portal. In 1998, Brian Garlick records the consequences and revises his reading of the inscriptions.

Contents
  • The discovery
  • Crossings and disaster
  • Sources and credits

The discovery

Brian Garlick says the SOLAR team found inscriptions and artifacts in desert ruins. They interpreted the material as instructions for building an obsidian portal. The testimony does not identify the excavation with the specific Egyptian complex.

Crossings and disaster

Explorers were sent through the portal, and creatures were brought back. Some team members suffered nightmares and changes. Garlick mentions some explorers being sent to the asylum.

In Final Notes, dated August 10, 1998, he describes a shock wave, collapsing sectors, and dead colleagues. He concludes that the inscriptions were a warning and asks that the facility be destroyed before the creatures reach the surface.
