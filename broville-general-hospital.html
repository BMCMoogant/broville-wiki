The urban hospital

Broville General Hospital, or BGH, occupies an imposing building in Uptown. Its interior contains reception areas, wards and patient rooms, operating rooms, and a morgue.

Chests contain themed medical items, including healing potions renamed as medicine and empty vials.

The asylum stands outside the urban core and has a separate body of records; it is a different property from the general hospital.
