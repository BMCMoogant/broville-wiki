Where to begin

Edward Page’s diary is in the canals bookstore. It is the starting point for following the bookseller’s investigation into a hidden space on the property.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

The passage and elevator

The wall behind the bookcases conceals an elevator shaft. Gates block the bookseller’s route, but old plans reveal a connection to the sewers. Edward reaches a rusty gate through the sewers and opens a way through.

Brosco’s confession

The destroyed elevator car at the bottom of the shaft connects the discovery with Brosco’s confession. He says he saw Rosswell armed, concluded that SOLAR wanted to kill him and released the emergency brake. He then locked himself in the laboratory.

Edward decides to return without an escort. His diary ends before the next descent, without recording the outcome of that decision.
