Using and editing the wiki

Read and explore

Read without an account. Use search, the Atlas and related articles to explore. Click images to enlarge them inside the wiki. Quest solutions remain behind spoiler controls.

Suggest a change

  1. Choose “Suggest a change” on an article.
  2. Explain the correction or addition. Include a book, sign, screenshot reference or source when possible.
  3. Send it for administrator review. A suggestion does not change the published article automatically.

Contribute

Use Suggest a change to prepare a GitHub Issue. Suggestions are public and require a GitHub account. The maintainer reviews them in a local editor and publishes updates.
