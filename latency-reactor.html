Also known as the Lag Generator, the machine helped sustain the old city and survived the disaster associated with it.

The old city's Lag Generator

The Latency Reactor, also called the Lag Generator, was designed to supply abundant energy and matter. According to the Doctor, it drew these resources from the world itself. Its use in H.I.V.E. transformed the old city's commerce, industry and entertainment. The researcher holds it responsible for the sinking of Broville. An emergency sign describes a radioactive meltdown, and the first report records that the machine survived and was removed from the epicenter.

The recovered machine and the experimental model

SOLAR was founded to study the machine's remains after the tragedy. Its name expressed the hope of a ray of light for reconstruction. The Doctor saw the reactor as a means of restoring the city's economy. His team settled in the western forest and refined technologies found in the recovered machine. Sales of these devices in New Broville funded research, and demand prompted the construction of a factory beside the laboratory. The third report distinguishes this work from an operational model that still generated excessive toxic Latency.

Sector C of the western complex retains signs identifying a generator test and the Unicorn Land elevator. The signs locate experimental activity on the map. The sequence of recovery, sales and model development comes from the Doctor's reports.

Environmental effects and related research

Community descriptions associate the name Lag Generator with environmental alterations: notes from the old mine levels say that blessings offer little help against Latency effects, and mention impaired long-distance vision and difficulty breathing, as if the chest were being compressed. Research facilities also retain mechanisms bearing “May Cause Lag” warnings. These references describe effects and signage without establishing that every mechanism is the recovered reactor.

Reactor technology appears within a research trajectory that includes the Chr0NOS temporal manipulation engine and dimensional portal studies, with references to the Nether and the Void; these projects are not a single machine.

Containment and disposal

The team needed to channel the excess somewhere it could be isolated. After opening a passage to the Void, the scientists made testing whether that plane could contain reactor radiation one of their objectives.

Form 8A-1, bearing a 1998 copyright, recommends sample X as a refrigerated insulator for channeling radiation into disposal zones. In tests, it rapidly absorbed electromagnetic and Latency-induced radiation. An addendum warns of a level 5 or greater breach if cooling fails. Management ordered work to continue despite the warning. Whether the Void could receive the excess remains a research question in the sixth report.
