The bow in the crypts

Witherbane is a bow kept in the cemetery crypts at −686, 57, 1355. The object’s own description presents it as a fabled bow once used to seal away the great demon. This association forms part of the story told by the object.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

Bearing of Doom, a book with no identified author, is found at the same location as the bow. It warns the adventurer against yielding to promises of power and riches. It then gives directions using a blue marker and graves to locate where the great being sleeps. The route is covered in The Cemetery’s Three Keys.

The sealing is also the subject of My Final Words, the testament of warrior Willam Brotori. He says that he put the great demon into a slumber, fears that the seal is temporary and leaves his belongings to the hero who finally defeats the threat. The testament does not name the bow.
