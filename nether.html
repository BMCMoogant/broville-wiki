Netherspawn and the imperial revolt

The Nether is the dimension associated with the Netherspawn appearing in CRN065's version of the imperial revolt. This reference is part of the account of the conflict.

Portals and research

Portals constitute another topic in SOLAR's experiments. The mention of the Nether in the imperial account does not establish a connection between those events and the Chr0NOS tests or Garlick's portal.

This is the final log of research specialist Brian Garlick, dated August 10th, 1998. If you find this please do not let my mistakes repeat again. I beg you

Page 2

I don't know how I've survived this long and how I got away. They were just, um, torn apart by those.. things. My friends... Oh dear God, what has happened to us? It was like a shockwave. At first it felt like the entire facility was going to explode.

Page 3

Entire sectors were caved in. Then lights went out and that's when the screaming started. I knew those portal experiments would be the end of us. I know we were desperate, SOLAR was going to go under unless something radial came about.

Page 4

In the end everything was my fault. We knew the ancient ruins out in the desert had more then their tresure in gold and jewels. There was knowlege of a long past civilization that possessed technology that may have surpassed even our own.

Page 5

So our salvation came or so we thought. what we gleand from the artifacts was something truely incredible. IT WAS ALL THERE, plans, schematics, the complete blueprint to create a portal to other dimensions. To think a civilization more then 1000 years old could create this!

Page 6

Even the mathmatics were right on point and all that was needed was a frame of pure obsidian and a jumpstart source of power. The expeditions showed a new world never before seen with anyones eyes. We brought back samples, maps..

Page 7

and... creatures from this new world. But even in the begining something was wrong. All of the explorers to some degree developed severe pyschosis and even complete insanity. They complained of night terrors and even seeing dead loved ones a relatives when awake.

Page 8

The ones that were not immediatly terminated were sent off the Broville Asylum. God only knows what happend to them. When we saw the warning signs it was already too late and now this has happened.

Page 9

It is only now the true scope of the horror we have unleashed has settled in. This Nether dimension is.. HELL and those creatures we brought back are infact ...deamons

Page 10

Now we know what happened to that once ancient civilization. The inscriptions were not instructions to build a portal. It was a warning That we completely overlooked!

Page 11

If only we didn't rush in our excitment of our new discovery, if only we took just a little bit more time to truely understand what it was they were trying to tell us. None of this, none of this would have ever happend.

Page 12

If you are still reading this, destroy this place entirely. If they ever reach the surface it will be the end of everything. I am fully guilty for everything happened here and now I will pay for it.
