Swamplands

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A network of shallow water, channels and wooded islands at the city’s northern edge. The ruined castle and Cattail Town guide exploration through a large, sparsely occupied region.

Terrain and crossings

The swamplands form a relatively uniform region of flat, wooded islands and water dotted with lily pads. Almost as extensive as the city, they are nevertheless among the map’s least occupied areas and mark its northernmost region. Cattail Town and the ruined castle are landmarks for crossing the area. The asylum lies in the forest west of the flooded ground; the road map helps distinguish the routes.

The settlement

The settlement consists of small single-story houses, a store, a church, a graveyard and a swimming hole. Its cluster of buildings is readily recognizable from above. An exploration account describes ordinary provisions rather than special rewards: fish, potatoes, bread, fishing rods, iron swords, full leather armor sets, boats and places to sleep. It also mentions large quantities of negative-effect potions that can be brewed in ordinary Minecraft.

Beneath the gravestones are coffins and urns containing bones and bone meal. A ladder in the church descends to a crypt with more bones and an unfinished wither-summoning structure. Behind a house, another ladder leads to a passage labeled as a black-market transport route. The explorer describes it as a route without loot or information, ending in an unfinished dead end.

Manor, temple and vessel

A ruined manor stands at the southern edge of the swamplands. The account praises the appearance of its interior but describes few spaces or objects beyond the facade: a central cave leads nowhere further, and the second floor contains an iron sword for anyone who manages to reach it.

East of the settlement, a spire rising above the water marks an underwater temple, described as having no loot. Nearby, a steamer beached against an island offers some supplies and beds.

Cemetery and traditions

The large cemetery contains builders’ heads in its graves. The exploration account recommends visiting on Peaceful difficulty. Beside the riverbank, a dungeon contains lever puzzles and a good bow at the end.

The castle is the landmark for Heraldus’s expedition. The Black Mother belongs to a separate tradition associated with a ritual in the swamps. Assessments of loot and difficulty come from one person’s exploration and may vary with the route and version.
