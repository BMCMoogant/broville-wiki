Sources and credits

This encyclopedia

Moogant conceived this wiki and leads its research and editorial organization. The Minecraft map was created by Oldshoes and the Broville building team.

World records

Articles draw on books, signs, items, maps and images from v11.4.4, alongside creator statements and archived production discussions. In-world history is distinguished from map development. Interpretations are identified where they matter.

Community material

Material from the Broville Fandom wiki has been integrated and expanded in the articles. The references at the bottom of adapted pages link to the original revisions and contributor histories. Adapted text is available under CC BY-SA 3.0. Image captions link to file information and credits; individual file pages do not always specify an image license.

The panorama was supplied by the wiki project owner. The Broville wordmark uses Bangalor by Yuji Adachi, as on Oldshoes’ website.
