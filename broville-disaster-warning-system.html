Purpose of the BDWS

The Broville Disaster Warning System, abbreviated BDWS, is presented as a long-term plan developed and maintained by Compass LTD. Its purpose is to evacuate the Broville metropolitan area in the event of an apocalyptic-scale disaster.

Planned infrastructure

Once completed, the system was intended to include several signal towers and evacuation tunnels directing the population to the airfield associated with the international airport. The description concerns a project in development, rather than a network whose completion is confirmed.

Project suspension

The account attributes the suspension of work to a class-action lawsuit against safety-vest manufacturers. Continuation would depend on the payment demanded in that dispute, narrated in hostile language toward those seeking payment. No date for resuming work is supplied.
