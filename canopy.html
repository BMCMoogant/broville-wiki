Canopy

OrganizationsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A company whose records describe experiments with matter and the B-Virus. The community history of the Black Line connects it to railway funding.

Canopy and the Black Line

The community wiki attributes most of the funding for the Black Line, built between 1919 and 1923, to Canopy. Presented as public transport, the line reportedly served primarily wealthy residents because of its fares. The community account lists a Canopy Corp station among the abandoned stations. Its exact location remains unresolved.

Experiments of July 7–9, 1998

Variant S-146-B

In the first record of Inorganic Sentience, variant S-146-B derives from 24 grams of magma cream injected with B-Virus. The mass began generating heat about two seconds after exposure and moving about two seconds later. During the first experiment, the heat melted the container. The team applied liquid nitrogen and placed the sample in refrigerated storage.

Variant S-146-A

On July 8, the team applied B-Virus to a mixture of two parts moss and one part pig brain tissue. The mass began moving after four seconds, assumed a cubic shape, and moved by hopping. Left in a container overnight, it doubled in size. The next day, it was frozen with liquid nitrogen and divided. When thawed, the fragments resumed moving independently and were frozen again.

Alghus Vinto's study

In Research, Alghus Vinto reports that his team had spent days studying a fragment received at the laboratory. They removed an eye-like structure that appeared inactive, while two other samples showed signs of life. Vinto describes a small purple organism resembling a worm, apparently living in the material, and announces animal testing. Another copy, attributed to darkfox118, contains the same account.

The Green Sector outbreak

Date of Outbreak records an outbreak in the Green Sector on October 23. Scientist, catalogued at the same location, −1055, 28, 704, lists Gregor Grendel as the head and Kao, Nawon, and Chirb as team members.

Unresolved questions

The origin of B-Virus, the source of the material studied by Vinto, and the year of the outbreak remain unknown. The books from Canopy's laboratories also leave open whether the S-146 variants, Vinto's samples, and the outbreak belong to the same chain of events.
