Entries

From July 7 to 9, 1998, Canopy monitors two samples. One heats up until it melts its container. The other assumes a cubic form, grows overnight and remains alive after being divided.

Location

The notebook is at −1059, 30, 615. The Collection brings together other books from the laboratory.

The two variants

The July 7 entry identifies S-146-B as 24 grams of magma cream injected with B-Virus. Within seconds it generates enough heat to melt its container and begins moving; the researchers cool it with liquid nitrogen.

On July 8, S-146-A is described as a mixture of moss and pig brain tissue. After exposure to B-Virus it forms a cube and moves by hopping. By July 9 it has doubled in size. When the researchers freeze it, break it into pieces and thaw those pieces, each begins moving independently.
