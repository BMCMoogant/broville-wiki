Camp in the cemetery

Darius Brolo is an adventurer who set up camp in the cemetery, drawn by the promise of a fortune. His search is followed in The Three Keys.

The progression of the journal

Darius begins his cemetery expedition hoping to free a wealthy, powerful prisoner and receive a fortune. His journal gradually changes from practical observations about graves and passages into an increasingly personal devotion to the figure he calls his Master.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

He observes a coven carrying a dark skull and learns that two more keys are needed. He finds another key in an open grave east of the bridge, but is driven away before retrieving it. As the search continues, he complains of emptiness and hunger and begins treating the prisoner’s release as an obligation.

His attempt to steal the coven’s key fails. The final entry sends him back toward a tomb he had previously been too frightened to enter. It records his decision to proceed, not the eventual outcome of the visitor’s quest.

Read original document — Darius' JournalDarius Brolo · 15 pages · Spoilers

Page 1

I've decided to set up my basecamp here. This cemetery is crawling with the dead; but I seem to be okay over here for now. The rumors about this place are certainly true, so then I am optimistic about the other rumors attatched as well.

Page 2

Whispers abound of a rich and powerful man that was wrongfully imprisoned here long ago. Because of his great power he was able to put himself in a deep slumber and await his freedom. The person that releases him shall be rewarded with great fortune!

Page 3

Among the dread of this place I stumbled upon a Witch Coven. They were in some ritual, they kept exclaiming that they have found a key. A key to what I wonder; the shackles to the imprisoned? It looks like I'm not the only one here looking for reward.

Page 4

I was able to get a better look at this key they found. At first it looked like nothing more than a skull. Yet the coloring was different, it was dark. After spying on it for a few moments I could feel a strange coldness in my heart. What is this key?

Page 5

Not being around the key the Coven has makes me feel empty and alone. Yet I cannot get too close to it because of those hags. I overheard them proclaiming they need two more. I shall beat them to the punch and find them myself!

Page 6

What luck! By sheer chance I stumbled into an open grave and found a key! Sadly I had to leave it there as I became over run by the ghouls of this place. I will have to go back. I marked its covering to stand out. It is to the east over the bridge.

Page 7

I haven't been able to retrieve my key yet, it is still not safe. He will surely be displeased with my failures. I will have to look for the final key for the time being, but where to start? I suppose I will try to find any grave that stands out.

Page 8

There are many graves, and few that differ. It is hard work, but I cannot falter now. My Master needs me. He will surely reward me for my loyalty. I will free him and he will make me rich and powerful!

Page 9

In my search for the keys I have come to realize I have neglected to search for the actual prison that holds my Master. I hope he will forgive this transgression. There is an opening near the bridge, I'm not sure where it goes. Perhaps there?

Page 10

I fear I am too cowardly. Once I made one step into that hole I stopped dead in my tracks. I was overcome with fear! Such terrible noises and smells came from within, I could not go further! Forgive me Master!

Page 11

I found another tomb of sorts. This one isn't nearly as terrifying as the other. Unfortunately the passage is blocked. There is a door, but I was unable to discover its method of passage. No matter, I do not feel the need to go there for some reason.

Page 12

If I cannot enter the tomb and find the third key, then I need to do something! I shall take the Witch's key! Those damned hags, they mock me! They flaunt their desire to serve the Master. I will show them that only I am worthy of such things!

Page 13

BLASTED WITCHES! I curse them! They foiled my plot to take their key. I was lucky to escape! They dare oppose me! I will show them! When I free my Master, I will ask of him to destroy them first! They are not worthy to serve him! No!

Page 14

The Earth.. it quakes. I eat my filling by my stomach.. I feel starving. I feel alone! My Master.. he is calling to me. He needs my help! I must answer his call! I cannot abide this terrible feeling anymore, he will cleanse it!

Page 15

I must go to the tomb from before. I have no other choice. Perhaps there I will find him, or at least a clue that will aid me in his freedom! I am coming, my Master!
