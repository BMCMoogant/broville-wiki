Traces beneath Downtown

Far below the surface, beneath the tower, one sign warns of the reactor's radioactive collapse and another orders people to keep away from H.I.V.E.. Reconstruction above these remains is narrated in the earliest reports by the Doctor.

The sinking and the catacombs

The story of v10 presents a metropolis predating v11, lost to the depths after a catastrophe at H.I.V.E. The community account describes months spent recovering bodies and the adoption of other means of disposing of the dead because of the scale of the losses. The survivors are said to have rebuilt the city.

Former workers, elderly by the time of modern Broville, reportedly claimed that v11 was built over catacombs containing the dead of v10 and that ruins existed even beyond them. The passage was supposedly lost as the city expanded. These particular catacombs and their access remain unconfirmed testimony, distinct from the signs and ruins identified underground.

The reactor and its recovery

The account of the fall describes a device intended to provide unlimited redstone power to the city. Its instability caused severe lag downtown and in surrounding areas; scientists kept pushing its limits until it reached a critical state, followed by implosion and the loss of the ground beneath the city. The full mechanism is partly redacted in the account, and no date is given, on the grounds that the records were lost in the sinking.

The Doctor's books attribute the catastrophe to the Latency Reactor and describe his own team recovering the machine, then establishing a laboratory and adopting the name SOLAR. This differs from the community version that attributes H.I.V.E.'s funding to the Feels Good Man Pharmaceutical Corporation, its design to SOLAR, and the recovery of parts to a later archaeological expedition, after which the company supposedly purchased or improperly obtained them. That same version describes a larger second reactor, left incomplete when the company dissolved, with an unknown purpose and location; these claims do not replace the sequence narrated in the books.
