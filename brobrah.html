Foundation and product

In A BroBrah Was Born, Don Bestick says he conceived a men's bra with a massage function while his firm was in debt. In the founder's promotional account, bank loans funded recruitment and the construction of the headquarters in four weeks. The company began manufacturing equipment and improvements for its own facilities, reducing its need to hire SOLAR.

The collider

Bestick says he purchased the Giant Hardon Collider when SOLAR ran into difficulties. He attributes commercial expansion from Melonport to Redlight City to the vibrating core developed with this technology.

The Collider Administration Manual identifies BroBrah as the owner and describes its operation. A BroBrah Certified Master Keycard unlocks the controls. The operator launches two Boost Shuttles and follows their progress through the lights on a scale model. The manual instructs the operator to retrieve the particle from the shaft after the collision, which it describes as highly radioactive, and to call the cleanup team.

Facility No. 3 and the prototype

In a message to supervisor Gaffer Honcho, Bestick states that the boxes marked as the final order complete the assemblies in Section A of Facility No. 3. He orders the section's workers dismissed and access blocked.

Undercover Log offers another perspective on the company. Paul Raten, working undercover as Paulie Ratzingo, receives an initial assignment to steal a BroBrah prototype. The diary leaves the prototype's function and its connection to the collider unresolved.
