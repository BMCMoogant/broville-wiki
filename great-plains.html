Region and excavations

The Great Plains appear in descriptions of archaeological work associated with the Museum of Natural History. The sites are treated separately from the general characteristics of this open terrain.

In the New Archeology Division's description, Site A lies in the center of the plains and Site B to their west. The former is associated with a hill surrounded by monoliths; the latter with the study of an ancient Celtic village.
