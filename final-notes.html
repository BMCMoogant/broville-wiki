The record of August 10, 1998

Brian Garlick identifies himself as a research specialist and writes after escaping the collapse of sections of a facility. He describes something like a shock wave, the loss of lighting, and colleagues being killed by creatures. The cataloged copy is at −261, 81, 2012.

The portal experiments

Garlick attributes the team's haste to SOLAR's financial situation. Researchers read artifacts from desert ruins as schematics for reaching other dimensions. In their initial reading, a frame of pure obsidian and an energy source were all that was needed to begin operation.

He associates the crossings with severe crises among the explorers, who reportedly suffered night terrors and visions of dead relatives even while awake. According to the author, those who escaped immediate execution were sent to the Broville asylum.

The warning

After the collapse, Garlick comes to read the inscriptions as a warning and asks that the facility be destroyed to prevent the creatures from reaching the surface. He blames himself and writes that he still sees them in the shadows. The text ends in fragments.

The date marks Garlick's final record. His testimony leaves open the exact identity of the ruins, the nature of the other side of the portal, and the immediate cause of the shock wave.
