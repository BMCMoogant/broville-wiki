FoxCreek Properties

OrganizationsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

Founded in Jamesopolis in 2009, FoxCreek Properties grew from small jobs into major construction projects in Broville. It is presented as a predecessor of D & F Holdings.

Foundation and relocation to Broville

FoxCreek Properties was founded in 2009 by a group of entrepreneurs in Jamesopolis, where it had considerable influence over the city government. Its history jokes about the rumor that CEO DarkFox118 was related to the mayor: they were the same person, using a different skin, with glasses and a hat as a disguise.

With the recession of 2011, DarkFox118 resigned as mayor and closed the local business. After a short vacation in Atlantis, FoxCreek arrived in Broville and established a temporary office at Sunny Bro Trailer Park. Its later building forms part of the city's commercial geography, and the company is associated with D & F Holdings as a predecessor.

Growth and the University Square office

Early work consisted of small jobs: interior buildouts, road construction, and similar services. In summer 2012, the startup Brotech Web services hired the company to rehabilitate a dilapidated warehouse near Brompton. The contract was negotiated and construction began promptly; by early 2013, Brotech could move into the completed property.

The project opened the way to larger construction work in Broville, including subway stations, public schools, and land development for the city government. University Square, where the office was located, is a major example.

Projects and responsibilities

FoxCreek's involvement ranged from complete construction to partial buildouts and property management:

  • Broville University, in conjunction with Brocorp property development.
  • Brotech Web services.
  • BanHammar Tower: lobby and access stairway by Mentalgen Construction; remaining interior buildout, occupancy and lease management by FoxCreek Properties.
  • FoxCreek Deco Apartment homes.
  • Foxcreek Domicile Apartment homes, described as under construction at the time of the account.
  • FoxCreek On the Square Luxury Apartments, also under construction in that account.
  • Johannes Gutenberg Public School.
  • University Station.
  • Unnamed subway stations, then under construction.
  • Sunny Bro Trailer Park.
  • Bro Street Diner.

Announced plans

In the absence of a statement from the CEO, marketing and public relations head Edward E. Edwardington announced the intention to improve residents' lives through new residential, commercial, industrial, and public-sector projects throughout the city. His statement ended with a humorous request to stop calling the office so the team could work. The announcement records plans without confirming the completion of future projects.
