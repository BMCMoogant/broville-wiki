The creature in the pipes

The Toilet Rat is humorously presented as a predator that lives in the pipes, waits and may leap from the depths of the toilet to bite flesh from its user. The community hypothesis that it is a biological aberration arising from paranormal and toxic proliferation after the v10 disaster is not demonstrated by the manual.

The manual's instructions

The text frames using the bathroom as a matter of survival and spiritual protection. Its five pages contain a sequence of warnings, a ritual and a final notice:

  • The first forbids sitting directly on the toilet because it might crack or break. It recommends hovering or standing directly over it, avoiding direct contact.
  • The second warns that the Toilet Rat sometimes awakens and leaps out to bite the user's flesh. The manual calls this unavoidable and immediately says it can be prevented, a contradiction that forms part of the joke.
  • The third instructs the reader to chant three times: “GREAT TOILET GOD, GUIDE ME THROUGH THIS JOURNEY OF CONSTIPATION SO THAT I MAY NOT BE SLAINE BY THE EVIL TOILET RAT! HAIL THE TOILET GOD!” This is followed by one clap to receive the blessing.
  • The fourth completes the promise: through the Toilet God's blessing, the Toilet Rat will be unable to harm the user.
  • A notice on the fifth page says the toilet has been permanently clogged by the GREAT DUCE foretold in past legends. Unclogging it would invoke the Toilet God's wrath, so the toilet is declared unusable.

The instructions belong to the book's humor rather than an encounter with a documented outcome.

PROPER TOILET USE: WARNING: Toilet is very fragile. DO NOT sit directly on toilet as it may crack or break. Attempt to hover or stand directly over toilet and avoid direct contact with toilet.

Page 2

Sometimes the TOILET RAT may awaken and leap from the bowels of the toilet in an attempt to bite off your flesh. This is unavoidable. However, it can be prevented.

Page 3

Simply chant "GREAT TOILET GOD, GUIDE ME THROUGH THIS JOURNEY OF CONSTIPATION SO THAT I MAY NOT BE SLAINE BY THE EVIL TOILET RAT! HAIL THE TOILET GOD!" three times and clap your hands once. This will grant you the blessing

Page 4

of the Toilet God so that the Toilet Rat will be unable to harm you.

Page 5

NOTICE: Toilet has been permanently clogged by the GREAT DUCE foretold in legends past. The toilet can never be unclogged without invoking the wrath of the Toilet God and thusly the toilet is no longer able to be used.
