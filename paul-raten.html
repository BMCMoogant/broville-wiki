Undercover identity

Paul Raten uses the name Paulie Ratzingo in an investigation involving a BroBrah prototype. His activity is associated with the BBI.

The assignment

Undercover Log identifies Raten as the agent chosen by the BBI to infiltrate a crime family operating from Legitimate Establishment LLC near the stadium. He adopts the identity Paulie Ratzingo. The diary follows his advancement through the organization rather than presenting a completed police case.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

The first recorded assignment is the theft of prototype trousers from BroBrah. On day 43, Raten says he has been formally admitted to the family. By day 187 he has reached its inner council, but suspects that the promotion lets the leadership watch him more closely.

His final entry concerns a planned shipment of cheaply made crafting tables. It leaves his operation in progress: the agent suspects exposure, but does not report an arrest, an escape or a confirmed discovery of his identity.
