Location and architecture

BN Tower stands beside Boardwalk beach near the urban waterfront. Its entrance is at the corner of 6th St. North and Vertigo Blvd. The tower is described as the city's tallest building, rising above cloud level and serving as a skyline reference both within Broville and on approaches by sea and air.

Construction and reputation

Engineer Metalus of Metalus Industries built the tower against Mayor Oldshoes' wish that no building rise higher than BroCorp's pinnacle. The local history jokes about the building surviving the mayor's wrath and includes a macabre remark about people of all races and genders jumping from it to their deaths.

Transport

On the subway diagram, BN Tower is an interchange between the Purple and Red Lines near the Boardwalk area.
