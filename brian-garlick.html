Research specialist

Brian Garlick signs Final Notes as a research specialist. He places the desert expedition at a time when SOLAR, according to his testimony, needed a breakthrough to survive. The team believed that artifacts from a civilization more than a thousand years old recorded technology comparable to their own. Their initial reading treated the inscriptions as instructions for a portal with an obsidian frame.

The explorers and the asylum

Garlick connects the crossings to severe changes among the explorers. His account mentions night terrors and waking visions of dead relatives. Explorers who, he says, were not immediately executed were sent to the Broville asylum. The researcher states that he does not know what happened to them after the transfer.

A changed interpretation

On August 10, 1998, Garlick writes that he escaped after a shock wave, structural collapses, and the deaths of colleagues. He reinterprets the inscriptions as a warning and believes that the hurried team ignored their authors' intention. He blames himself for what happened and leaves a request to destroy the facility. His fate after the final record remains unknown.
