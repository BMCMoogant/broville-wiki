Dates within the world

  • 1089 and 1909 appear among the first stops of Chr0NOS.
  • 1924–1925 are the years covered by the trench diary.
  • 1929 and 1934 appear in sanatorium patient files.
  • In 1983, a SOLAR leaflet recruits volunteers. In December of that year, Dondiego Allen writes in the mountains.
  • In July 1998, Inorganic Sentience records Canopy's tests. On August 10, Brian Garlick leaves his final account.
  • April 2013 is the month of the BVPS expedition.

Sequences with uncertain placement

In the Doctor's account, the sinking precedes the recovery of the reactor and the rebuilding of the city. The disaster's year remains unresolved. The ancient peoples and Heraldus likewise lack firm dates.

Community dates

The 1919–1923 dating for the Black Line and 2002 dating for SOLAR's bankruptcy come from community articles. They appear with that attribution in the corresponding articles.

Map publication

v10 was released in 2011. Construction of v11 began on new terrain in August 2012 and ended in November 2016.
