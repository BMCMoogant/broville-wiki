War in the trenches

The diary's authors change as different combatants occupy the position. A stranger searches for an orange rod, a detail connected to the search for cores along the Chr0NOS route.

CRN065's testimony

He says the war was an internal uprising, with Netherspawn supporting the anti-imperialists. His letter describes a camp where the regime enslaved and killed its own inhabitants. He states that partisans would assassinate the czar and that the communist state came afterward.
