Imperial War

EventsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

The czar's war is remembered in the trenches of 1924–1925. CRN065 claims that the opposing forces were inhabitants of the same country.

War in the trenches

The diary's authors change as different combatants occupy the position. A stranger searches for an orange rod, a detail connected to the search for cores along the Chr0NOS route.

CRN065's testimony

He says the war was an internal uprising, with Netherspawn supporting the anti-imperialists. His letter describes a camp where the regime enslaved and killed its own inhabitants. He states that partisans would assassinate the czar and that the communist state came afterward.

May 14th 1924 The enemy has started its recent push. Nothing hefty, just the usual peasant patrol. They think they can run our government. Those bastards.

May 22, 1924 They've cut us off! We have no way to return to command without heavy casualties! We have no way to tell how long it will take for reinforcements to arrive. It may be weeks... maybe months...

May 26, 1924 The man who writes this diary is dead. I am now in command. Down with the Czar, and curse his name to the Nether! Long live the cause of freedom!

Page 5

August 5, 1925 Our regiment retook this valley today, and I found this journal in the enemy trenches. Glad to see it returned to the Commonwealth. I wonder what the original owner was tasked to find... Will update shortly.

Page 6

August 7, 1925 We managed to find something! It seems to be an iridescent orange rod... However, a strangely dressed man approached the camp, asking if we had found something similar. My captain forcibly removed him.

Page 7

August 7, 1925 (cont.) The stranger came again tonight. MUCH more disturbed than earlier... He held my captain at gunpoint. We had no choice but to reveal what we had found. The stranger described it as a key.

Page 8

August 8, 1925 A key to what? That's been plaguing our minds since yesterday What is so important about it I wonder? I will follow the stranger tonight to see exactly what will become of this strange object.

Page 9

August 9, 1925 The man who has followed me is dead. Player, I have hidden it where only you can find it. There is no good or bad side in this war. Only the need to survive our harrowing journey. I will meet you soon.

Page 10

November 1, 1925 How has this journal gotten into my chest? We need to fortify our trenches to prevent this mystery from getting into our heads further. Although the men have said they recently saw this stranger again.

November 1, 1925 Shoot on sight is in command for anyone without a uniform of the Czar's service. This is the final entry. -Cpt. Beryov
