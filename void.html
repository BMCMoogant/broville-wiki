Void

ConceptsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A plane between time and space studied by SOLAR. Books from the mountains describe its effects on materials and records.

From mountain tradition to the laboratory

A colleague told the Doctor that monks at a temple in the southern mountains had opened a passage during a spiritual quest. The place was described as a plane between time and space. In the fourth report, SOLAR engineers briefly accessed it. In the fifth, they widened the portal and collected a small sample.

The sample and the reactor

According to the Doctor, the material emitted dark energy and, at subzero temperatures, worked as a good conductor for the portal. In a separate record, form 8A-1 describes sample X as an insulator for channeling Latency radiation to disposal zones, provided it remains refrigerated. The two books describe different functions. The form identifies the material only by the code X.

The testing program

The sixth report lists three objectives: discover whether the Void was empty, observe its effects on living beings and test whether it could contain reactor radiation. To observe participants, the team built modular chambers with physical and mental obstacles. When volunteers ran out and profits fell, the Doctor authorized illegally obtaining people for the tests.

Living near the Void

In On the Effects of the Void, the keeper of records explains that water barriers offer some protection from the beings he calls hosts. Proximity to the Void, however, corrodes objects and causes everyday records to disappear. Local preparations delay the deterioration for a time. A copy is at −292, 6, 2656.

In Structural Conce, a devotee reports that the Void passed through the mausoleum twice and took bodies. Another manual describes a cage that briefly exposes its occupant to the Void for ritual purposes, linking the practice to the mountain cycles.

Open questions

The accounts allow comparison between residents' experiences and SOLAR's investigation. The connection between the underground community and the monks mentioned by the Doctor remains unresolved. Using the Void as a radiation dump appears as an experimental objective, without a recorded result in the series.
