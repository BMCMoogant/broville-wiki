SOLAR Bankruptcy

EventsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

The Doctor’s accounts show a company under financial pressure. Community chronology dates its bankruptcy to 2002 and records asset sales to Compass.

Contents
  • Decline

Decline

The Doctor's sixth report describes falling profits and the insistence on expanding the tests. The collapse narrated in Final Notes has its own date, in 1998.

What survived

Community chronology links the 2002 bankruptcy to acquisition by Compass. The accounts of Bestick and Fuchdix show how technologies and projects reached BroBrah and LightSource.
