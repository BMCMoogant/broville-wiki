A research and technology company founded in 2000 and connected to BroCORP in the community history. It acquired SOLAR assets and developed the project that became LightSource.

Foundation and acquisition

Community history presents Compass LTD as one of the companies connected to BroCORP, focused on research and development. It places its founding in 2000, with Dr. Steve, PhD, as CEO and MasterSteviekun as director. The same history attributes the acquisition of SOLAR assets and licenses to the company in 2002, after SOLAR filed for bankruptcy.

The accounts give the transferred assets more than one recipient. In A BroBrah Was Born, Don Bestick credits BroBrah with buying the collider when SOLAR was struggling. In the same promotional book, he calls for a boycott of Compass. The acquisition attributed to Compass should not be taken as proof that every piece of SOLAR equipment passed to a single company.

Divisions and activities

Compass Energy Partners

The energy division's mission was to supply Compass and SOLAR properties with uninterrupted power while implementing experimental sources developed by the research division. Regular supply and the application of new technologies therefore appear as parts of the same activity.

Compass Research & Development

The research and development division earned income from facility leasing and patent royalties. Successful experiments considered non-dangerous could receive consumer versions and reach the market through Compass Consumer Goods.

Community history also associates Compass and SOLAR with sponsorship of the Broville Bureau of Science and credits Compass with maintaining its public laboratories. The company is also responsible for the Broville Disaster Warning System, a project involving signal towers and evacuation tunnels toward the airport.

Products in the books

  • Compass T4-80: BrownTech's BT Catalog advertises this home computer for $199.99.
  • OptiServ 2000: the OptiServ 2000 Manual describes a steam-powered server. The machine was supposed to shut down if a piston jammed, preventing steam buildup. The manual acknowledges erroneous shutdowns caused by sensor failure and requires 45 hours of cooling before handling.
  • Chronoligical Display Unit: in OFFICIAL LETTER, MasterSteviekun invokes the warranty terms to reject a product recall. The letter satirizes the distinction between logic and the supposed ligic used to justify how the clock works.

The LightSource project

According to Frank Fuchdix, Compass revived a proposal shelved by SOLAR to automate the management of an entire company. The project took years and underwent revisions as hardware evolved. The team created LightSource to test the program in a communications company and kept the connection secret to protect Compass's reputation.

Frank's second diary attributes the removal of references to LightSource from Compass's research database to LightSource itself. He reports that, after being fired, he returned to the subsidiary's office and removed the rules preventing competition or legal action against Compass and its subsidiaries. The dates when the project began and the relationship broke down remain unresolved.
