Downtown

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A core of commercial towers east of Oldtown. Above the ruins stand the BroCORP tower and the station serving the central district.

The center above ground

The district plan places Downtown between Oldtown and the Arts District waterfront, north of Chinatown. The community description identifies three central landmarks: the BroCORP tower, the Metalus Industries building and Broville Bureau of Investigation headquarters.

Two stops for the center

On the Metro Map, Downtown is a green-line stop between Brocorp and China Town. Brocorp station, at −249, 46, 1806 in the package's reference list, provides an interchange between the green and red lines.

The city beneath the streets

In SOLAR's third report, the Doctor mentions an imminent inauguration ceremony for the BroCORP tower, built over the old city's ruins. The Atlas records two underground notices, at −174, 5, 1840 and −174, 5, 1841, concerning the H.I.V.E. facility and the radioactive meltdown. These points locate remnants of the sinking in relation to the station.
