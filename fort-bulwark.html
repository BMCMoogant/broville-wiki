Fort Bulwark

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A fortification in the far east, beyond the airport. The regional map includes it on the eastern transport corridor.

Contents
  • Mapping
  • Sources and credits

Mapping

The fort occupies the eastern extremity, beyond the airport peninsula. On the regional network, it is the last point shown after Portvale. Lieldrindre's altar stands in the ruins south of the military base.
