The collection

Beethoven’s lost symphonies are collectible sheets scattered throughout Broville. Their item names number a collection of 64 pieces. The search accompanies exploration of buildings, underground spaces and other corners of the world: each sheet adds a number to the set.

The v11.4.4 inventory located 62 distinct numbers. Pages 3 and 7 still have no confirmed location in the material examined. This describes the scope of the search without claiming that they are impossible to find in the world.

Musical references around the city

In the book The Beatles, the author says that Broville’s classical music critics rank Beethoven, despite considering him highly controversial, above other musicians popular in European courts. A sign complains in exasperation: “If you play Für Elise one more goddamn time...”. These details place the collection within the city’s humor and cultural life.

The earlier survey and expanded locations

Xeraction’s list recorded 42 sheets extracted from chests, leaving 22 numbers unlocated. It was a partial survey: sheets outside chests were excluded. The expanded inventory used here found 20 of those numbers and provides 62 locations in the table below.

The sign about Für Elise is at −350, 75, 1759. It is a humorous reference to Beethoven, rather than a clue to the remaining sheets.
