Chinatown

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

An area between Downtown, Arts District and City Port. Its name appears on the city map and the metro network.

Contents
  • Location
  • Sources and credits

Location

Chinatown lies on the eastern edge of Downtown, between Arts District and the port area. The green line terminates at China Town on the metro diagram.
