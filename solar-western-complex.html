The forest and surroundings

The complex's position west of the city corresponds to the laboratory described by the Doctor. Nuketown lies to its west and the asylum farther north. The maps mark all three as separate landmarks. The SOLAR scientific research facility is described as derelict.

Sector C

The Test Fire and Unicorn Land signs identify the generator test and the elevator. These points belong to the research and reactor area; Unicorn Land is covered in its own article.

Other sectors and addresses attributed to the company appear in the inventory of SOLAR facilities.
