Alpine Village

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A lakeside settlement in the snowy southern mountains. Broville’s tourist guide presents it as a ski resort, with chocolate and downhill slopes among its attractions.

Terrain and access

Alpine Village stands in a recess in the southern mountains, beside a lake. The railway map shows a dedicated branch to the settlement, which provides a landmark between the high ground and the valleys.

Tickets called Alpine Rail Express Ticket identify a one-way journey to Alpine Village Rail Station. Signs in the world also name the village’s station and railway.

Mountain tourism

Warp Recommendations, a guide published by tourist publisher BroTours Publishings, describes Alpine Village as a ski resort far from conflict and, jokingly, from basic comfort as well. It recommends trying the chocolate, sliding down the slopes and attempting to stay warm.

For visitors starting on Fractal Island, the guide locates the travel access to the village along the winding road that climbs the mountain south of the starting point. These directions concern the departure point on the island.
