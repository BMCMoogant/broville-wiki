A major corporation present in Broville’s commerce and cityscape. According to the SOLAR reports, its tower was built over the ruins of the old city.

The tower during reconstruction

In SOLAR Report [#3], the researcher known as the Doctor writes that the inauguration of BroCORP Tower, built over the ruins of the old city, was scheduled for the following day. He regarded BroCORP as a competitor while SOLAR expanded its laboratory to manufacture technologies derived from the Latency reactor.

Commerce and identity

Warp Recommendations, a guide from tourism publisher BroTours Publishings, presents the tower as the headquarters of a large company involved in trade between the city’s businesses. Community accounts also associate BroCORP with GameBros and PizzaBros.

The slogan “Bros Building Buildings, Bro” accompanies its corporate image. Its humorous introduction claims that the company was founded more than 9,000 years ago and guided every version of Broville; this hyperbole supplies no usable date for the history narrated in the reports.

Business relationships and interests

The LightSource Information leaflet includes BroCORP among the businesses served by LightSource, an advertising and communications company. Its offering includes targeted advertising, telemarketing, bulk messages and employee notifications. The advertisement names BroCORP as a customer but does not specify which services it purchased.

A Diary signed by Brocorp Agent, an agent of BroCORP, reports that his superiors believed a group of hermits possessed something valuable. The writer did not share their enthusiasm. His brief account neither identifies the object of interest nor records the company obtaining it.

Secret Agenda presents the 2014 plans of a secret society called the Fraternal Order of the Block. Signed by Grandmaster Architect, the agenda includes paying BroCORP management to forget an excavated tomb. This is an intention recorded by the group, with no confirmation that the payment took place.

A portrayal of the corporation

In The Shocking Tru, Phil Dudley, who introduces himself as a doctor of cryptozoology, describes BroCORP as a holding company: a business that holds stakes in other businesses. He credits it with dozens of subsidiaries in the major economic sectors and places its rise in the mid-twentieth century. This description belongs to a conspiratorial pamphlet; the extent of corporate control and the chronology alleged by its author are not confirmed by the other documents cited here.

Finding the headquarters

On the Metro Map, Brocorp Station connects the green and red lines. Its reference in the package list is −249, 46, 1806. The guide to Fractal Island, the starting island, also identifies a teleport point to BroCORP in the tallest office building north of the island’s starting point.
