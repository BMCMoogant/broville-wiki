Island, port and resort

Road Map places Brolapagos southwest of the urban center, in Broville’s western ocean. The package’s reference list marks the island at −2155, 70, 2496. Its port settlement and the Lizuli Azure resort and spa form its tourist-facing side. The resort is presented as one of the city’s premier exotic destinations.

The old monastery stands on the other side of the island and is described as one of the first buildings erected by settlers after their arrival. This landscape of port, accommodation and religious mission conceals a more violent history, recounted in its founder’s testament.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

The testament My Final, at −2157, 72, 2372, preserves Bembe Coto’s version of the colonization. He admits that his group killed the inhabitants they were trying to convert and buried their bodies and cultural remains beneath the mission.

The Atlas records the Old Spanish Key one block below the book, at −2157, 71, 2372. The testament connects the clue to the mouth of the water and asks for the key to be returned to him. The monastery quest follows this testimony.
