From authorization to filming

On April 3, 2013, plumber and paranormal investigator Jameson Hawks records permission to enter the asylum. He saw recording a show as an opportunity to change careers. In the same diary, he says the group had already been interviewed by the local newspaper The Broville Beat and secured a television deal. The team arrives on April 10 with cameras, lighting, and a generator, under Mike Host’s direction.

Division of work

Recording combines camera observation with audio capture. Trina Brendel monitors the material while Jameson and Wilson Harolds move through the building. Wilson also handles power and access. Eric, who Wilson says joined the group only a few weeks earlier, and Tim appear in their colleagues’ accounts.

Recording and editing

In the second diary, Jameson reports that the cameras had been installed in the dormitory and that director Mike Host proposed spending some time there that night. The plan was to leave some cameras recording during the day and return that evening to collect them.

Jameson describes little activity during this initial observation beyond possible voices in the recordings, noises and particles. At the same time, he acknowledges that editing could make the material more frightening. His account thus distinguishes field observations from the intended effect of the show.

The team’s accounts

The diaries alternate between authors, times, and locations. The authorization entry and field accounts show how the visit was planned and then conducted. The sequence of events appears in the asylum expedition.
