Canals District

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A canal district south of Uptown and west of Brompton. The former station converted into a bookshop preserves the connection to Brosco’s laboratory.

Geography and access

The district map places Canals south of Uptown, east of Broto Lake, and west of Brompton. Warp Recommendations identifies the cathedral as a landmark for exploring the neighborhood.

  • Canals East brings together the red and blue lines and the yellow connection to Breno Monorail.
  • Canals West lies between River Valley and Breno on the regional railway diagram.
  • At −276, 47, 2139, a sign marks access to the Black Line.

A station converted into a bookshop

In Journal, found at −507, 70, 2228, Edward Page recounts opening the bookshop in a deteriorated station. Investigating machinery noise, he found an elevator shaft behind a bookshelf. A confession by Dan Brosco is recorded nearby, at −496, 60, 2224.
