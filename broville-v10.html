The 2011 edition

Oldshoes dates the city's creation to 2010. The v10 page on Planet Minecraft is dated August 16, 2011, and yumstheman opened a release discussion on Minecraft Forum the following day. Looking back in February 2014, Oldshoes placed the end of work on v10 around autumn 2011. The publication date and the approximate recollection of work ending mark different milestones.

Oldshoes' page presents a city to enter and explore, with interiors, treasures, and secrets. Its gallery preserves views of the bay, buildings, and interiors. Captions and notes identify the Coterie Craft texture pack and renders made with Chunky, two factors to consider when using the images to recognize the appearance of blocks.

What players found

Surface, transport, and underground spaces

On August 24, 2011, Pin_Collector described exploring the map in the release thread. The account mentions docks, ships, a railway, catacombs, and the player respawning in the hospital. It also mentions an ice rink that melted in the player's game, an effect the player attributed to a mod. The account places a waterfront, rail transport, and hidden spaces beneath the city in the same map.

Pikimon_2 reported performance problems in the same thread and suspected the large number of signs in The Hive. The message documents that name and the presence of signs in that player's experience; the explanation for the slowdown was the player's conjecture.

A precedent for v11

In the 2014 demo presentation, Oldshoes listed v10-style housing among the neighborhood types in v11. The comment documents an explicit architectural reference. In the same discussion, Fangride recalled sewers, an underground crypt, shops, and skyscrapers, but placed those memories in v10 or an earlier version. The 2011 account provides the most direct attribution of the catacombs to v10.

Files and provenance

  • Oldshoes' publication. The original page is the reference for authorship and date. During the recorded inspection, its main button returned to the page itself without delivering a verifiable file.
  • Release post referenced in 2011. On the forum, yumstheman directed readers to a specific post on Oldshoes' Tumblr. The contemporary reference preserves the historical route, but the inspected post did not provide a verifiable package.
  • Skeep_Redinho's conversion. On the v10 page, Oldshoes reproduces the conversion author's account. The author used Minecraft 1.7 and WorldEdit to fix chests and paintings disappearing when upgrading the world and reports that some doors remained cut off. The package is presented as a separate copy adapted for 1.8 onward. Its compatibility label describes that adaptation.

The old city in v11 accounts

The sinking is told in v11's books as the disaster that befell the previous city. The name The Hive in the 2011 thread provides a point of comparison with H.I.V.E. in later reports. Attributing specific characters, mechanisms, places, and events to the two editions requires comparing the contents of their respective worlds. See From v10 to v11 for the revision of the story described by its creator.
