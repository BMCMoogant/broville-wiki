The released maps and the alternate reality

v10 and v11 are separately built worlds. v10 was released in 2011. In August 2012, Oldshoes began an entirely new map to take advantage of terrain generation and larger biomes. v11 was completed in 2016. In the history told within v11, the old city sank and rebuilding began.

In December 2019, in the "Secrets" channel of Broville's Discord server, Oldshoes described v11 as an alternative version of v10 and discussed revising continuity. This supports reading v11 as presenting its own past, with records about the desert and the Broviet Union that can be studied within that world. The alternate reality hypothesis goes further: an incident at H.I.V.E. might have created this reality, including its past. The mechanism remains open. The new map and revised continuity are production facts; the origin of another universe is an interpretation of the fiction.

Ancient peoples and medieval legends

In the far west, the Egyptian complex preserves a city, monuments, and funerary objects. In the jungle, a diary describes traces of the Alkali clan. In Brolapagos, Bembe Coto’s testament recounts how a religious mission buried the culture it encountered beneath the monastery. In the mountains, the dwarven tradition is associated with the Arkenstone. These peoples have independent records and uncertain dates.

A ruined castle marks the approach to the swamp where Heraldus’s diaries place his expedition. The king and his knights descend in search of an artifact said to grant strength and vigor. The story continues in Eye of Arburaz and the expedition, with spoilers separated.

Empire, revolt, and the Broviet Union

The trench diary records fighting in 1924 and 1925. CRN065, a traveler working for SOLAR, says schoolbooks concealed the war’s origins. The inhabitants were fighting one another. In his final letter, he describes a prison camp and announces the death of Czar Broseph III and the state that would follow, known as the Broviet Union.

The Black Line reveals another aspect of the old city: an underground rail network. Community tradition dates its construction to 1919–1923 and associates its funding with Canopy. In Brompton, the station and housing illustrate how urban growth left structures that would later be reused in other ways.

Sinking and rebuilding

H.I.V.E. used the Latency Reactor to supply energy and matter. Notices found beneath downtown record a radioactive meltdown. In his first report, The Doctor accepts his part in the disaster and recounts how the city sank. The machine survived, and the team recovered it.

The Doctor moved research to the forest west of downtown. The laboratory became SOLAR, selling technology to finance its work. Meanwhile, BroCORP opened its tower over the ruins. The exact date of the disaster and its position relative to episodes such as the Black Line remain unresolved.

The laboratory era

The reactor continued producing excess Latency. An account of mountain monks led SOLAR to the Void, a plane between time and space that the scientists began studying. The company opened test chambers and recruited volunteers; by the Doctor’s last report, it was kidnapping people to sustain the program.

In 1998, Canopy and SOLAR left records of different projects. While Canopy studied samples that began moving and growing, Brian Garlick recorded a collapse following portal experiments. Some explorers affected by the expedition had been sent to the asylum. The 2002 date for SOLAR’s bankruptcy comes from community company accounts.

The city after SOLAR

Compass began using designs SOLAR had shelved. LightSource, a company run by an automated system, exemplifies how these ideas survived. BroBrah purchased technology from the struggling company. In the canals district, a bookshop occupied the former station connected to Dan Brosco.

In April 2013, the Broville Paranormal Society entered the asylum to record a show. Its diaries provide access to a more recent story on the same property. The mission article places the documented events and resolution behind a spoiler heading.

Chronology and reading the evidence

1089 and 1909 are destinations in Chr0NOS. The years 1924 and 1925 come from the trench diary. A SOLAR leaflet dates to 1983, laboratory reports record 1998, and the BVPS diaries date to April 2013.

The ancient peoples, Heraldus’s expedition, and the sinking occupy periods without a secure year. The history of the released map has its own calendar. Use the source collection to distinguish objects, character accounts, production statements, and community traditions.
