An abandoned property northwest of Concord Commons. Dormitories, a water tower, a crematorium, and the main building surround access points to underground levels.

The property

The Road Map marks The Asylum in grid square E8, between Cattail Town and the SOLAR complex. Concord Commons, to the southeast, provides an urban reference point on the subway network. The team's first diary from 2013 records the approach at −1320, 68, 1470. The dormitories, water tower, and crematorium are separate landmarks from the main building.

Patient records

The 1929 book records patient discharges, while the 1934 book lists admissions. G. Freeman appears in both. The admissions include M. McFly, E. Brown, and B. Tannen, a reference to Back to the Future. The records date the institution's activity, while the description of renovations in the 1930s comes from later testimony.

The connection with SOLAR

In Final Notes, dated August 10, 1998, Brian Garlick states that explorers developed severe symptoms after portal tests and that some were sent to the asylum. He explicitly says he does not know what happened to them.

The 2013 investigation

The BVPS returned to the property with permission to film. Its records distinguish the dormitory area, the floors identified by color, and the facilities beneath the building. The distribution of the volumes is described in the BVPS Diaries. The narrative route is covered in the investigation mission.
