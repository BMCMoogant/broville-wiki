Where to begin

Look for the Test Fire sign in Sector C of the SOLAR complex, by the generator or Latency Reactor test area. An elevator sign bears the name Unicorn Land.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

The safety door and the fall

Pressing the Test Fire button opens the safety door at the end of the corridor, revealing an elevator. As the player approaches, the floor in front of the elevator disappears. Tripwire hooks at the bottom of the drop activate a teleport to Unicorn Land.

The staged wheat field

The destination is an artificial wheat field, with clouds, scenery, and sky made of wool. A small hut stands in the center, and the player arrives directly in front of it. After a few seconds, another teleport returns the player to the actual surface.

The scene has been read as a reference to the end of Portal 2, where Chell reaches a wheat field by elevator, with the hut echoing the elevator room. The community account also compares the artificial setting to an unofficial sequel scenario in which the field is underground and represented by screens. These comparisons are interpretations of the scene, not events in Broville's history or a confirmed link to an official Portal sequel.
