The Doctor’s Reports

DocumentsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A series of six reports about the sinking, the founding of SOLAR, the Void, and the testing program.

Contents
  • Reading order
  • Where to find them
  • Sources and credits

Reading order

Reports 1 and 2 recount the sinking, the recovery of the machine, and the new laboratory. The following reports cover the factory, the Void, and testing. The sixth records worsening finances and kidnappings.

The sequence presents these events from the Doctor's perspective and follows the formation and work of SOLAR.

Where to find them

A collection of all six volumes is at −2011, 64, 1741. The Document Collection identifies the books individually.
