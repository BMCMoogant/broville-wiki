Brosco's assistant

Rosswell was Dan Brosco's assistant. The events leading to his death are narrated by Brosco in A confession, in the underground laboratory.

The elevator

Brosco says that he saw Rosswell carrying a pistol on the cameras and interpreted his arrival as an assassination attempt ordered by SOLAR. After speaking to him over the intercom, he released the elevator's emergency brake while the assistant was in the car. According to the confession, the fall killed Rosswell; the text does not establish the company's alleged order.
