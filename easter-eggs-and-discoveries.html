Signs, jokes, and tributes

  • A sign says the cake is a lie, referencing Portal.
  • Another sign complains about the repetition of Für Elise.
  • Owls drawn in text appear across several neighborhoods and are among the most recurring jokes on hidden signs.
  • The desert pyramid contains the Quack of the Covenant joke.
  • Behind the asylum, a grave marked Tiny Tim references A Christmas Carol by Dickens.

Passages and hidden places

  • The Black Line is an abandoned metro line in the central part of the city.
  • Beneath BroCORP Tower lie the old city’s ruins, identified in community tradition as remnants of v10.
  • In Brompton, a friend shop has a button labeled press for friend that spawns a witch. The same neighborhood has a Hobo Haven beneath a metro line.
  • The sewers lead to numerous secret rooms.
  • At the far west of the map are an ancient city and pyramid.
  • A room beneath BroBucks Corporation’s main headquarters holds the secret of the ingredient in BroBucks coffee.

Machines and environmental humor

At the junkyard, a supercomputer calls the player fleashbag, a humorous spelling of “fleshbag,” when one of its buttons is pressed. Another supercomputer in the same location kills the player instantly when one of its buttons is activated.

Near Concord Commons, beside the remains of Progress Highway, stands a shack described as containing very offensive humor.

Collections and experiences

The symphony collection spans several regions. The SOLAR generator conceals Unicorn Land. The adventure at the altar of Lieldrindre has its own article with a spoiler warning.
