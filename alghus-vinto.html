July 1998 research

In July 1998, Alghus Vinto records the analysis of samples that include an eye-like object and purple organisms. His research is presented in the context of the Canopy laboratory.

The July 28 notebook

In Research, dated July 28, 1998, Vinto describes several days of work on an unusual material. The team has removed what resembles an eye from one segment, which appears inactive. He distinguishes it from two samples showing clear signs of life and describes a small purple worm-like organism in the material.

His entry ends with a plan to begin animal tests and an ordinary office detail: Bill from Flora has brought flowers for Vinto and Susan. The juxtaposition makes the experiment part of a staffed workplace, not an anonymous report of a completed disaster.
