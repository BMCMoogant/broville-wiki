The transition between maps

On the forum in 2014, Oldshoes explained the physical separation between v10 and v11. The project begun in August 2012 adopted a different world to take advantage of new terrain and larger biomes. In the demo, the team still used v10 as a stylistic reference for some houses. There is therefore a documented design relationship independent of the fictional disaster story.

What the creator revised

In the "Secrets" channel of the Broville Discord server, on December 17, 2019, Oldshoes described v11 as an alternative version of v10 and treated the change as a retroactive revision of continuity. In the Solar channel on June 20, 2020, he said SOLAR had been retroactively incorporated into v10. On April 25, 2022, he described the revision from H.I.V.E. to SOLAR. The messages are in the local collection in secrets-page-2.txt, beginning at line 1156, and solar-page-1.txt, at lines 1031 and 330. These are comments about the writing of the story.

Reconstruction within v11

The first SOLAR report attributes the sinking to the Latency Reactor. The Doctor accepts responsibility, describes reconstruction attempts, and records the machine's recovery from the wreckage. In the second report, he recounts residents moving to villages by the lake while his team established a laboratory west of the city and adopted the name SOLAR. A set of these reports is cataloged at X −2011, Y 64, Z 1741 in v11.4.4.

This sequence connects the old city to the new one from the Doctor's perspective. Emergency notices preserved beneath downtown, at X −174, Y 5, Z 1840–1841, offer another starting point for investigating the disaster. The 2011 discussion of The Hive and these v11 remains belong to different editions and documentary contexts.

A past of its own and the alternate-reality hypothesis

The books in v11 describe events with their own chronologies. The trench diary records fighting in 1924 and 1925, and the accounts of CRN065 offer another perspective on the war and the formation of the Broviet Union. In Final Notes, Brian Garlick links portal technology to the knowledge of an ancient civilization. These episodes form part of the past narrated by v11. The chronological relationship between the sinking and several of them remains open.

The alternate-reality hypothesis proposes that the H.I.V.E. accident created another world with its own past. A variant imagines a world that already existed and received the city. The revision described by Oldshoes explains how the team could write a different past. The Doctor's reports describe catastrophe, recovery, and research, leaving unresolved what mechanism, if any, might have altered reality.

After v11

In the 2016 interview, Oldshoes expressed skepticism about his team making a v12 and mentioned the possibility of a smaller fantasy project. The maintenance releases listed on his website retained the v11 numbering and updated the map for newer Minecraft versions.

Meanwhile, Puhelias presented her own continuation on Planet Minecraft, with a demo identified as the August 15, 2022 save and a plan for two cities, Broville and Brotowa. She also published Brork Island as a prototype and later indicated that the island would be excluded from the final project. These publications document a community initiative and the scope announced by its author.
