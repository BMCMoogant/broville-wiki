The west-coast refuge

Squatters Court is the destination sought by Hobo Ed, a member of HoBro, a brotherhood living in the city’s hiding places and passages. In Exodus, his evacuation notice, Ed identifies the site on the west coast as a place where fugitives may find other members of the group.

Leaving Broville

In the notice, Ed states that Hobo Joe, called Emperor Joseph, has been killed. As second in command, he declares himself the new leader and orders everyone to leave the city. He connects this decision to other recent deaths and the disappearance of the Court of Rails, another group associated with the underground rails.

Ed believes that someone has discovered how to reach the hiding places and threatens their residents. The book presents this explanation as his suspicion, without identifying whoever is responsible.

Route and uncertainty

The escape described passes through sewers, hidden doors and cracks unnoticed by homeowners, heading toward the west coast. Ed writes that the group is seeking its companions there and qualifies his directions to Squatters Court with a warning: the refuge may have fallen as well. The notice confirms neither their arrival nor the destination’s safety. The evacuation is presented in The HoBro Escape.
