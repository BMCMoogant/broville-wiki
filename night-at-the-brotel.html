Night at the Brotel

DocumentsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A romantic science-fiction and fantasy novel attributed to LightSource, set in a Broville hotel where two men fall in love at night.

Authorship and setting

Night at the Brotel is presented as written and published by LightSource. The author's real name is not revealed; the suggestion that it begins with D because that is the letter of love is a joke in the book's presentation.

The narrative combines romance, science fiction, and fantasy in urban Broville. Two lovers try to conceal their forbidden love while discovering more about themselves and each other.

Synopsis

The mysterious G works alone at night furnishing the interior of a new hotel called The Brotel. One night, another mysterious man, D, interrupts his work. Their meeting is described as love at first sight, but the synopsis leaves open whether everyone sees the relationship the same way.

Reception within the fiction

The promotional presentation claims the book received critical acclaim on its release night, outsold all of Descole's books combined, and became the world's most famous book, with a copy in every home. These are humorous exaggerations in the work's fictional reception.
