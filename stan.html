Isolation in the forest

Stan flees to the forest out of fear of a new reactor. His diary describes decades of isolation and presents his assumptions about what might be happening in Broville; those ideas belong to the perspective of a resident cut off from the city.

The birthday entry

Diary of Stan is written as a birthday reflection after what Stan calls twenty years in the woods. He says that an attempt to recreate the reactor doomed the people near SOLAR and that he escaped before the disaster.

Stan imagines Broville as an abandoned, creature-infested city. He is describing what he believes the distant city must look like from his isolation; the entry does not present that imagined scene as an eyewitness visit to present-day Broville.
