The campus

Broville University, or BroU, is presented as the main university in v11, serving more than 3,000 students. Its extensive grounds include a multistory classroom building, dormitories, a pool and a cafeteria praised for its food.

The campus is a landmark for University Square and nearby transport access. University Station and Campus are the names of separate stops, useful for navigating the university area.

Student rivalry

Most students are described as taking part in a healthy rivalry with neighboring Hapsland Uni. This relationship appears as a feature of local academic life.

Inquiries into graduating classes

The community account mentions several inquiries over the preceding two decades into graduating classes that might have direct links to Broville's criminal underworld and Hip. S. Ter. This is framed as a suspicion, without identifying here which classes or what conclusions were established.

The Broville Education board recommends avoiding those classes, completing the satirical portrait of the institution and its connections beyond the campus.
