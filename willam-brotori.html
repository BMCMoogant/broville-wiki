The challenge left behind

Willam Brotori claims to have sealed away a great demon and leaves his possessions to whoever completes the task. His account connects with the search for the cemetery’s three keys and Witherbane.

The bequest in My Final Words

Willam’s short message says that he has sealed a great devil into slumber but is dying from his wounds. He fears that the seal will only be temporary. He addresses a later hero who will have to confront that danger and leaves his belongings to that successor.

The book is therefore both a warning about the seal and a bequest. Its address to a future reader should be distinguished from a report that the reader has already completed the task.
