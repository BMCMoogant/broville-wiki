Abandoned Manor

LocationsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

An abandoned property in western Broville’s swamp lowlands, also called SOLAR Manor. The identity of its former resident is unknown.

Contents

  • The former resident
  • Sources and credits

The swamp property

The manor rises above the flooded ground and waterways of western Broville’s swamp lowlands. It is introduced as SOLAR Manor and with the phrase “Qua Patet Orbis Et Argentum”. The property is derelict and had been abandoned years before the account.

The former resident

Its name and clues found inside the house gave rise to the rumor that it belonged to an eccentric scientist or even the founder of SOLAR Corporation. The description does not identify who lived there or confirm either possibility.
