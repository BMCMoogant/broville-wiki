Uptown

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

An urban area between Oldtown and the canals, near the center. Its location helps orient visitors heading to Edward Page's bookshop.

Orientation

Oldtown and Downtown lie to the north, University to the west, and Canals District to the south. The hospital and Twin Pines Mall are associated with Uptown in the community collection.
