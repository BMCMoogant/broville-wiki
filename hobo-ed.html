The withdrawal order

Hobo Ed belongs to the HoBro brotherhood. After companions disappear, he orders a withdrawal. When the city’s hiding places become dangerous, he seeks refuge at Squatters Court on the west coast.

The proclamation in Exodus

In Exodus, Hobo Ed introduces himself as Edmund J. Abernathy Jr. and claims the titles King of the Caves, Lord of Sewers and Duke of the Rails. He describes himself as the late emperor’s second-in-command and declares himself acting Emperor of Vagrant Kind under the brotherhood’s written rules.

The withdrawal is his first order. He cites repeated killings and the disappearance of the Court of Rails as reasons to abandon the city’s hiding places. His message directs anyone seeking refuge through sewers and concealed passages toward the west coast and Squatters Court.
