Context and starting point

Hobo Ed's HoBro is an underground fraternity. Its account concerns losses among its members and should be read to follow the decision to leave the site.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

After Joseph's death and the disappearance of members of the Court of Rails, Hobo Ed orders an evacuation and intends to seek refuge on the west coast. The diary ends with the order, without confirming arrival. SOLAR abducted homeless people, but the fraternity's account does not identify Joseph's killer.
