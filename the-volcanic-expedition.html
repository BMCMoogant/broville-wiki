Personal Log May 07 Earlier today the professor and I went up to the summit to make an investigative dive. He needed to verify the unusual readings the monitoring equipment gave him, so up we went with the diving suits.

Page 2

After hooking him up to the emergency winch I lowered him down into the Earth's molten opening. A few strained minutes passed before my radio crackled to life, his disembodied voice urging me, "you need to see this".

Page 3

I drew the winch back up and rode its tether down into the sweltering, impossibly orange depths. The magma almost completely obscured my vision; anything more than a scant few inches away fadded into the blazing haze.

Page 4

Despite that, I managed to find an opening in one of the tunnel's walls and I stumbled through it. I found the professor waiting there on the other side of the hole, and beyond him laid an ominous sight. Masonry the color of dried blood interspersed

Page 5

a large, hellish chamber in a maze-like fashion. The very top of a stark white building could be seen in the center of the chamber, looming above the labyrinth below. A moan emanated from around a corner as a lumbering figure came into view,

Page 6

no doubt one of the maze's nightmarish denizens. There was a lot of panicked shouting, running, but in the blur of confusion I managed to find the end of the winch tether again before those creatures got to me. I've been separated

Page 7

from the professor, but it's far too dangerous for me to go back down there by myself to look for him. We weren't equipped to deal with something like this, what am I to do? For now, I've lowered the winch tether in case he makes it back to the entrance.

Page 8

I've called in a boat, it should be here in a few days. Whatever's lying under this island, it's not safe to stay here. I only hope he makes it back soon.
