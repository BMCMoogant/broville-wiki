Numbering and authorship

The collection includes entries signed by Jameson Hawks, Mike Host, Wilson Harolds, and Trina Brendel. Jameson opens the series with the April 3 authorization and records the April 10 arrival. The number 15 identifies two texts by Jameson, preserved at different locations. Volume 16 is by Mike and dated April 11, earlier than several volumes numbered before it.

Reading locations

  • The first volume is at −1320, 68, 1470 and the second at −1344, 72, 1471.
  • Volumes 9 and 10 share −1420, 90, 1517. Volumes 12 and 13 occur at −1419, 54, 1540.
  • The two texts numbered 15 are at −1458, 53, 1465 and −1449, 53, 1497. Volume 16 is at −1446, 18, 1504.

Copies of several volumes exist outside the property. The document collection indexes consultation points. The reconstruction comparing times and testimonies appears in the mission.
