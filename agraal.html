Agraal

CharactersEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A gold-armored creature who possesses the Eye before Heraldus’s expedition. The playable encounter presents a manifestation named Agraal’s Vengeance.

The figure on the throne

Heraldus’s expedition describes a very tall being with elongated limbs, golden armor and a black sword. Smoke escapes through the armor’s joints when it moves. The diary initially describes its appearance; the name Agraal comes from the voice of the Eye of Arburaz, which identifies him as its former captor.

The quest tradition associates Agraal with the destruction of the Brotherhood of the Vagabonds and the confrontation with Heraldus, in which the knight Wilhelm was severely wounded and Agraal’s body was defeated. The explanation that his spirit survives to protect the Eye belongs to the community interpretation of the subsequent encounter, distinguishing the diary character from Agraal’s Vengeance, the boss fought by the player deep within the swamps. The account and exploration are brought together in Heraldus’s quest.

Combat and statistics

The encounter uses a scripted mechanism with two creatures stacked together, acting as a single unit, and waves of reinforcements. The black sword belongs to the narrative description; the playable entity uses the enchanted iron sword listed below.

Reinforcements and effects

  • Two ghasts are summoned when the boss activates.
  • Waves of skeletons wearing iron and leather armor act as guardians.
  • A lightning strike, technically identified as LightningBolt, and portal and lava particles mark the appearance.

Location and activation

The command block summoning the boss is at −1603, 9, 1393. Combat begins with a message from the “Booming Voice”: “What audacity! Again you come to steal what is rightfully mine. I will teach you a lesson you won’t soon forget!”
