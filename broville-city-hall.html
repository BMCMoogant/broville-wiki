Administration and location

Broville City Hall stands in Downtown, above The Palisade business center, and is presented as the center of municipal public policy management. It houses the office of Mayor Oldshoes, also described as chief executive of BroCORP, the largest local firm. The satirical characterization of the mayor says that he never visits his office and delegates almost all responsibilities to his deputies.

Facilities

The building includes:

  • A reception area and several administrative offices.
  • A staff lounge, meeting room and space dedicated to foreign relations.
  • A reception hall, guest rooms and a dignitary’s suite.
  • A rooftop garden.

Assembly and tower

City Hall also houses an assembly responsible for the city’s legal matters, whose motto is “Sic Sember Cannabis”. A tower flying the Broville flag rises from the top of the building, watched over by a tower keeper.
