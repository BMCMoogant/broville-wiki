The disappearance record

Ranger Rich is the ranger who recorded a camper’s disappearance and the statements collected afterward. His account forms part of the investigation into the Braulding disappearances, in the camping area.

How the reports change perspective

Friday’s Investigation opens with Rich being told to preserve his report as a witness in a police investigation. He recounts the arrival of eight campers, their first report of a theft and the subsequent disclosure that a sister had disappeared. Rich repeatedly interprets events through drinking, unreliable testimony and the effect of the woods on visitors.

Today’s Investigation adds a witness from a western campsite. She describes a woman with brown hair and blue pajamas who said she was determined to go up to the light. This echoes the phrase recorded in Abby’s Camping Diary, but the two documents tell it from different viewpoints.
