Franchises and prefabricated structures

Indeterminable Developments Konglomerate, or IDK, brings together franchises designed to be bought out later by other businesses. It also operates as a construction company specializing in customizable prefabricated frames.

Business model

The model described consists of overdeveloping an area until unfinished projects make the place unsightly to nearby residents. This situation creates an incentive for business owners to take over the incomplete construction, paying a price significantly higher than the lot’s original value.
