The jewel and the dwarves

The Arkenstone is described as a jewel with sharp points that shines with its own inner light. According to community tradition, the Dwarven Society discovered it long ago in the heart of the Broville Mountains. Considered priceless, the stone is said to have vanished along with the dwarves.

Search clues

The search text advises visitors to be patient, listen to mountain echoes and the howls of demons rising from the depths, and follow the tracks of men who desecrated the ancient lands. These are evocative exploration clues rather than a detailed sequence of events.
