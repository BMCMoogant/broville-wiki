Latency

ConceptsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A phenomenon exploited to generate energy and matter. The excess produced by the reactor is a central risk in the reports.

Nature and production

Latency, commonly called “Lag,” is described in the community account as an invisible, intangible substance produced as a byproduct of energy transmission. Widespread redstone use creates localized pockets that are usually harmless to players and server integrity; large redstone computers can accumulate far greater quantities. The account describes unrefined Latency as highly toxic, associating even small exposures with hallucinations and player timeouts.

The Doctor describes a machine capable of supplying both energy and matter. The H.I.V.E. reactor and the v10 catastrophe connect the exploitation of this phenomenon to the destruction of the old city. Later reports focus on excess Latency and the need to isolate its radiation.

Exposure and containment

Community descriptions associate prolonged or intense exposure with paranoid delusions, and mention workers on systems such as LightSource reporting that software was becoming conscious; that association alone does not establish the cause or truth of their experiences. Servers saturated with Latency are also described as capable of inflicting severe burns if cooling periods are ignored.

SOLAR investigates a sample associated with the Void and keeps it refrigerated. Lab Report Form 8A-1 describes sample X as absorbing localized radiation, including Latency-related radiation, and warns that loss of cooling could cause a breach of level five or higher. Its properties resemble the material discussed by the Doctor, but the form does not identify its origin.

Operating precautions

The community compilation of SOLAR and Compass manuals identifies these precautions:

  • Use only SOLAR fuel rods to maintain energy stability and limit Latency residues.
  • Allow servers to cool for 45 hours to avoid physical injury and system malfunction.
  • Prepare to repair generators manually during catastrophic failures if SOLAR customer support is unavailable.

The community list of associated organizations also expands SOLAR as “Scientific Observation & Latency Analysis Research”; the company's name elsewhere is SOLAR Applied Sciences Inc.
