Read original document — LightSource Log 1Frank Fuchdix · 9 pages · Spoilers

Page 1

LightSource is an experiment to fully automate the accounting, resources, stock management, and inventory of an entire company, automatically. The experiment was started in 2\,\-, however the idea for the experiment is much older.

Page 2

The very idea for the experiment was brought up by Ro-d F-\h\y back at SOLAR when it was still in business. However, without the proper resources to make a sufficiently advanced artificial intelligence, they idea was little more than a pipe dream and was

Page 3

not explorered by SOLAR, as they were investing their resources into more immedate matters, like interdimentional transportation. Fast forward a decade and suddenly computers are a lot faster and a lot cheaper. That's when Compass found

Page 4

the old documents for the idea and decided it was really worth checking into. So they did. It took quite a few years, and constant setbacks, since once they got a decent beta, the whole computer field changed and they had to change a lot too.

Page 5

Finally, the the computer field relaxed, and LightSource had been totally stabalized. We didn't want to test it on Compass, since we didn't want to run it into the ground with some faulty programming, so we established LightSource,

Page 6

subsidary of Compass and the same name as the software. Though we kept the subsidary part privet just in case LightSource went crazy and made PR so bad for itself it stunk up it's parent company.

Page 7

Anyway, so we established LightSource as some generic "PR" company, really it just sends spam email and solves captchas. We gave it a little office, hooked up some computers, gave it some cash to pay it's bills and locked it up.

Page 8

I have to say, monitoring LightSource, it's done some... interesting things. I'm not shutting it down, since I don't really know if it's gone rogue or if it thinks those are legitimate business decisions that will further itself. Even if it did go rogue,

Page 9

if it's not hurting anyone, it'll be interesting to see for development purposes. - Frank Fuchdix, Compass AI Team
