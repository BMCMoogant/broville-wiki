Read original document — Explorer's LogDigby Braveman · 5 pages · Spoilers

Page 1

July 14 -------------------The remaining jungle ruins of the Alkali clan are few and far between. I've been scouring deep within this dense jungle for weeks, yet the largest and most intact ruin I've seen yet has been near the shore all along!

Page 2

July 14 cont. -------------------This stairway is very intriguing. Many strange human remains (mostly skulls) litter the connecting cave. What could they mean?

Page 3

July 15 -------------------Upon careful examination of the skulls, I've found that they were forcibly removed from their bodies. They are human and notably bear the small dents caused by traditional Alkali body modifications.

Page 4

July 15 cont. -------------------Given these findings, did this stairway once lead to a prison? I dare not dig deeper until I can secure the decrepit stone brick interior from collapsing.

Page 5

July 16 -------------------Searching every nook and cranny of this stairway has finally yielded a mechanical button. Oh, the temptation! Could it open a secret passageway? It's right out of archeological fiction! Maybe I won't have to dig after all!
