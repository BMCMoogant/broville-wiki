From the interior to the far east

On the Rural Commuter Map, the route from Coal Mine passes through Nuclear Plant and reaches the junction west of River Valley. From there, the eastward sequence is River Valley → Canals West → Breno → City Port → Braulding → Portvale → Fort Bulwark.

The junction and mountain section

From the junction west of River Valley, another branch runs down to Space Center, on the southwestern coast. The Alpine Village section appears separately from the main corridor and ends at an unnamed point in the diagram.

Reference points in the city

Canals West belongs to the regional map. Canals East is the subway interchange. Breno appears on both the regional railway and the urban diagram, where the yellow connection leaves Canals East under the label Breno Monorail.
