Chr0NOS and imperial history

CRN065 is a SOLAR employee connected with the use of Chr0NOS. Through this technology, he shows the visitor concealed episodes of imperial history associated with the Czar.

The guide in the time logs

CRN065 addresses the visitor directly through four TIMELOG books. The first records a successful jump to AD 1089 and treats the search for replacement fuel cores as a puzzle. The second records AD 1909, where he says he has passed himself off as a redstone power engineer. The Chr0NOS manual describes these destinations as fragments of altered time-space.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

TIMELOG3 changes the purpose of the journey. CRN065 says he entered SOLAR’s program to investigate his suspicion that the company had concealed Broville’s past. He describes the imperial war as an uprising among the country’s own inhabitants, rather than a war against foreign invaders.

TIMELOG4 announces that he intends to remain in that time and cannot return the engine to SOLAR. His later letter, player..., describes a prison camp and a note hidden by a fellow prisoner. It also says that the Czar will be killed three months later. This is the narrator’s foreknowledge within the journey, not a date supplied for the modern city.
