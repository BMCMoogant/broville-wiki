In April 2013, a paranormal team arrived at the property to record a show. Its diaries allow visitors to retrace its passage through the buildings.

Where to begin

The first BVPS diary, at −1320, 68, 1470, records the authorization and provides a reference for approaching the asylum dormitories. The water tower, crematorium, and main building are points along the route. Compare dates with author names, because volume numbering differs from the chronology of the entries.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

April 10: recording breaks down

Jameson finds a body near the water tower. Mike insists on filming and, in the crematorium, activates the equipment with Eric inside. After the fire, the team becomes locked in the main building, with no phone signal, and loses sight of Mike. Wilson finds an elevator shaft and looks for a way to restore power.

April 11: Mike’s diary

In volume 16, at −1446, 18, 1504, Mike says Eric had arranged the job and expected 80% of the money. Mike retrieves the coal elevator key from the ashes, descends, and releases gas through the piping, which he says supplies every level, including laboratories. His assignment was to assemble the team and disperse the substance. According to the information he read, it could cause hallucinations. He expected to receive more than 2.5 billion dollars and escape to Brolapagos. A noise in the generator ends his entry.

April 12: diverging accounts

Trina says Tim returned shaken after exploring the orange level and spoke only of a decaying corridor. In the morning, Jameson records nightmares among the team and says they powered an elevator connected to the red level’s nursing station. Below it, Wilson finds an operating room, hears voices, and notices shadows in the observation area. His text becomes illegible. Jameson goes upstairs after a radio call and finds Trina dead. Wilson stops answering the radio, and Jameson sees bloody footprints while searching for Tim.

The final attempts to escape

Jameson finds Tim again in a shaft. An elevator that might provide an exit does not work for him. In the final volume numbered 15, at −1449, 53, 1497, Jameson thinks he recognizes Mike’s whistling and decides to descend the stairs. The books leave open who hired Mike, what caused each apparition, and Jameson’s fate after descending.
