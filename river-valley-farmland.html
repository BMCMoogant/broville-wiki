River Valley Farmland

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

Farmland southwest of the city. Its fields and roads mark the transition between the industrial district and the southern terrain; a tourist guide highlights the contrast between old equipment and large power installations.

Agricultural landscape

The cultivated areas of River Valley Farmland occupy the space between the industrial district, rural roads and the beginning of the southern mountains. The road map distinguishes the region from Rural Farmland, farther north.

In Warp Recommendations, a guide from tourist publisher BroTours Publishings, the region appears in the Farmlands section. The description contrasts old equipment with gigantic power-supply fans, presenting the rural landscape alongside large structures.

Arrival and railway

On Fractal Island, the guide identifies a farmhouse west of the starting point as the travel access to River Valley Farmland. The sign at this departure point also names the region as its destination.

On the mainland, signs identify River Valley Station and the directions toward Portvale and the space center. The station provides a landmark for consulting regional railway routes.
