The Study of the Void

EventsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

After learning about mountain monks, the Doctor seeks a way to dispose of excess Latency. The SOLAR team comes into contact with a plane between time and space.

The reactor problem

Excess Latency required isolation and a destination. A colleague told the Doctor about a passage opened by mountain monks, and the team brought back material that had to be kept refrigerated.

From reports to rites

SOLAR begins studying the Void in its facilities. The Doctor's reports follow this research. The records of the cycles present another perspective: that of a community living near it and dealing with its effects on books and objects.
