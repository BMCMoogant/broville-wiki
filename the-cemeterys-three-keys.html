Where to begin

The diary at Darius Brolo’s camp, at −715, 63, 1314, is the starting point. The cabin and bridge serve as surface landmarks in the cemetery. Read the diary alongside the local messages to follow how each author interprets what lies buried there.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

Darius’s search

Darius sees witches with a dark skull and hears them say that they still need two keys. He describes feeling cold near the object and empty when away from it. He finds another key in a grave east of the bridge, but flees the creatures after marking its cover, leaving the find in place. He also discovers an opening near the bridge and a tomb with a blocked passage. After failing to steal the witches’ key, he decides to return to the tomb. His diary ends when he has already begun calling the prisoner his master.

Instructions in the crypts

  • At −686, 57, 1355, look for Bearing of Doom and the Witherbane bow. The book warns against promises of wealth and power.
  • From the blue marker, count four graves south, five west and seven south. Dig the earth in front of the indicated gravestone.
  • At −653, 51, 1341, Art Thou Chosen? requests proof of the demon’s defeat before granting access to the sacred tomb.
  • At −633, 53, 1339, My Final Words accompanies the sword Willam’s Retribution. Brotori leaves his possessions to whoever completes the challenge he left behind.

What remains unresolved

The notes distinguish the witches’ key, the find east of the bridge and the third key sought by Darius. The adventurer’s last entry ends before revealing what he found when he returned to the tomb.
