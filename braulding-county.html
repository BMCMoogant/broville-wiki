Between the port and the airport

The district map places Braulding east of City Port, beside the airport. The regional station lies on the City Port, Braulding, Portvale and Fort Bulwark route. The package reference point, 327, 69, 2038, guides arrivals in the region. The ranger station at 450, 66, 2092 is the landmark for the campsites.

The investigation in the park

Ranger Rich’s two reports are kept together at the station. One was retained because the county asked him to preserve his witness account. The other records a visit by a camper from a sector to the west. Abby’s diary allows comparison between the official investigation and the experience of someone in the park. What the search uncovers is covered in The Braulding Disappearances.
