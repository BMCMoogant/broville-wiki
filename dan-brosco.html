Research and disappearance

Daniel “Dan” Brosco was a renowned physicist working for SOLAR in the southern Canal District. His company-funded research concerned interdimensional transport. He believed he was approaching a discovery that could save everyone.

According to Broville Beat files consulted by Edward Page, the old station and warehouse formed part of his laboratory. SOLAR placed him on forced leave, citing a psychotic break. He was last seen entering the laboratory, and the newspaper subsequently declared him missing.

Rosswell's death

In A confession, Brosco reports seeing his assistant Rosswell carrying a pistol on the surveillance cameras. Convinced that SOLAR had sent him to kill him, he taunted the assistant over the intercom and released the emergency brake while Rosswell was in the elevator car. The fall killed Rosswell, according to the account. The alleged assassination order is Brosco's interpretation.

The scientist moved materials, equipment and work from the upper laboratory to the lower level, sealed the emergency exit and locked the sewer maintenance access. He decided to remain isolated until he finished his research, preventing anyone from stopping him before it was too late. The nature of his discovery and his fate after the confession remain unresolved.

The laboratory and Edward Page's discovery

The complex occupies at least two underground levels beneath the station converted into Edward Page's bookstore. Its community location reference is −494, 69, 2239. The elevator shaft is hidden among the shelves.

Years later, Page found the shaft behind a bookcase and described a wrecked elevator car at the bottom. Old plans and permits led him to a sewer passage, where he managed to open a corroded gate. Thus, although the shaft is the hidden entrance described in the laboratory article, the journal also records this maintenance route. His journal ends with his intention to return unaccompanied. The route and reading locations are covered in The Laboratory Behind the Bookcase.
