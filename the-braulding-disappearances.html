Where to start

At Ranger Rich's post, read the two reports at 454, 65, 2090. The camp diary is at 503, 71, 2112. The Friday and Saturday entries help put the witnesses' and campers' accounts in order.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

The complaint and the woman in pajamas

The police call Rich about an alleged theft. The group's leader eventually reveals that his sister has disappeared and had said she was being followed. The search finds a note stating that she caught a ride back to the bar. Rich considers a drink-fueled misunderstanding plausible. In another statement, two campers hear a noise near a trash can around two in the morning and see a brown-haired woman in blue pajamas. One approaches and hears her say that she needs to reach the light. Missing-person posters prompt the witness to contact the ranger.

Abby's two texts

In Camping Diary, Abby remembers the woman's blank expression as she continued up the trail. She writes that Bailey Ann went to speak to the ranger and that she herself wants to investigate. In the second text, titled .;e and found at 483, 49, 2329, Abby says that she saw something outside the tent and regained consciousness in a freezer. According to her, the main entrance was sealed. She pretends to be dead when the creature brings another body and eventually manages to open the door. With a broken leg and screams in the corridors, she leaves a warning for anyone who comes afterward.

Unresolved identities

Abby never identifies her captor. Billy's diary offers another account of encounters with campers but does not settle the creature's identity. The origin of the light and whether Abby left the complex remain unanswered.
