Ranger station and camping area

Ranger Rich's station is the starting point for a visit to the park in Braulding County, at 450, 66, 2092. The ranger's reports are at 454, 65, 2090. Abby's Camping Diary is at 503, 71, 2112. The regional railway helps locate the county before continuing to the station.

Accounts on the surface

The Friday report records the arrival of eight campers on Thursday at around 7 p.m. and the staff's departure at 10:30 p.m. The other report begins from testimony originating at one of the campsites to the west.

Abby's Saturday entry shifts the perspective from the ranger to a camper who decides to investigate on her own. These accounts introduce the search; its route and discoveries are covered in the Braulding Disappearances quest.

Reach of the investigation

The investigation that begins at the campsites leaves the surface and reaches a place of captivity. The mission route brings together the continuation of the diaries and the access points associated with that discovery.
