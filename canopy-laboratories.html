Research records

The Canopy laboratories are identified through records of samples, medications and biological tests. The documentation is associated with Inorganic Sentience and the B-Virus.

Separate experimental records

The notebook Inorganic Sentience records tests on two B-Virus variants on July 7–9, 1998. Its accounts cover heat generation, movement, growth and cold storage.

A separate Research entry by Alghus Vinto, dated July 28, describes an eye-like structure removed from a material sample. That segment appears inactive, while two other samples show signs of life. Vinto also records a small purple worm-like organism and plans animal testing. These are separate descriptions in the laboratory records, not interchangeable names for one specimen.
