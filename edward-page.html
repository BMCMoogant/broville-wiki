The station turned bookstore

In his Journal, Edward Page describes converting an old station into a bookstore. Machinery noises lead him to investigate an elevator shaft hidden behind a bookcase, with a wrecked car at the bottom.

Research and underground passage

To understand the property, he consults plans, old permits and Broville Beat news. The files connect the station and warehouse to the laboratory of Dan Brosco, a SOLAR researcher declared missing.

Page finds a maintenance passage through the sewers and manages to open a corroded gate. His diary ends with his intention to return unaccompanied; it does not record that return's outcome. The Journal can be read at −507, 70, 2228, and the investigation is covered in The Laboratory Behind the Bookcase.
