Where to begin

Read My Final in the monastery’s funerary area. The Old Spanish Key appears directly below the book, at −2157, 71, 2372.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

Bembe admits that the mission killed the island’s inhabitants and buried their culture beneath the monastery. He writes that he preserved something, points toward the mouth of the water, and asks for the key to be brought to him. His record ends with a warning about what might be in the depths.
