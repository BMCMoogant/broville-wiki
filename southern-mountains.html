Southern Mountains

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A snowy mountain range with villages, cabins, and underground sites. Some books preserve cycles, rites, and accounts of proximity to the Void.

The snowy southern range

The maps place the mountains south of the urban core. Alpine Village lies beside a lake in a recess of the terrain. Rural Commuter Map shows a separate line to the village, with its other end unnamed.

Records at different elevations

  • The cycle series is concentrated at Y 6. At −284, 6, 2650, Neleph Cycle recounts creatures emerging from the altar and going to the prison to collect an offering that was still being prepared.
  • At −292, 6, 2656, On the Effects of the Void describes the deterioration of objects and records and the use of water as protection against local beings. The Atlas also lists a copy in the collection at −2028, 64, 1736.
  • At Y 109, Sample B32 Incident is at −399, 109, 2713, and Sample Properties [Agricultural] at −393, 109, 2685. The first concerns a containment incident. The second records a sample's effects on plants.

An unresolved connection

The connection between this community and the monks mentioned by SOLAR remains unresolved.
