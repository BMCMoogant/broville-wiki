Where to begin

The reference point for a visit is the monumental complex, at −3115, 84, 2348. Use the canal and temple to orient yourself in the isometric view. The community description distinguishes the gallery entrances and treats the temple as a separate location.

Spoiler warningReveal solution and ending

The burial gallery

The first route connects the room containing gold and the staff to the sarcophagus chamber. Stick of xXAnubizXx is at −3043, 81, 2488. The skull and wrappings bearing the name xXAnubizXx are at −3044, 75, 2488. The item names identify the burial assemblage.

The bird gallery

The other route begins outside. The Quack of the Covenant sign is at −3042, 62, 2488, and the Goose item at −3041, 60, 2488. The bird figure and the sign's wordplay allude to the Ark of the Covenant. Their proximity on the map helps distinguish the joke from the named objects in the burial assemblage.
