City Port

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A port on the city’s eastern edge. It is the urban landmark before Braulding on the regional railway.

Roads and railway tracks

The port connects the city network with the eastern corridor. On the regional railway, it lies between Breno and Braulding. It is a landmark for distinguishing the port area, Brompton and the airport.
