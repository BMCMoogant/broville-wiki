How to consult the collection

Choose a subject or search by title and author. To understand each account in the order in which events are presented, begin with the relevant history or mission page.

Series and accounts

  • The Doctor's Reports: six volumes about the sinking and SOLAR's work.
  • BVPS Diaries: records connected with the organization's investigation.
  • Diary 1925: a notebook that passes between combatants at a trench position.

The document and series pages bring together reading references and the available locations for each set.
