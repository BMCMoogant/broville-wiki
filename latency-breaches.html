The release of Latency

A Latency breach is described as a critical failure of containment systems that causes the uncontrolled release of that substance or effect. Some accounts also allow that paranormal events may produce breaches. The map and its narratives present occurrences in urban, underwater, and mountainous settings.

Occurrences and associated places

  • Old city: a massive breach is held responsible for Broville’s sinking, also called the V10 Catastrophe. The H.I.V.E. notices and the Doctor’s reports provide their own accounts of the collapse.
  • Beneath the southern glacier: an underwater breach can be seen by using Broville University’s submarine, located near the airport. The community narrative associates it with the Broific Rim war, in which giant machines confront creatures from the ocean.
  • Inside the mountains: very ancient remains of a breach occur deep within the region associated with the dwarven society. The hypothesis that it caused that society’s end remains an interpretation, without a conclusive date or sequence.
