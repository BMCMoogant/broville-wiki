Community organization

The records describe guards, miners and people responsible for the archive. Ter-Seph, Neleph, Lelech and Chattur function as names of cycles, with no reliable conversion to the city's years. They are part of the vocabulary used to follow the life of the underground community in the mountains.

Rites and samples

Some books narrate offerings and creatures around an altar. Others follow samples that alter plant growth or escape containment. These accounts present both ritual activities and the observation and handling of unusual materials.
