Arts District

RegionsEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

A small waterfront district east of Downtown and north of Chinatown. Its name also appears in Black Line documentation.

Waterfront and transport

Arts District occupies a narrow stretch of coast east of Downtown and north of Chinatown. Documentation for Boardwalk Station associates the station with the arts area, the exhibition and museums.

The railway survey also mentions an old Black Line stop named after the neighborhood, Arts District Station.
