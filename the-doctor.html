The Doctor

CharactersEdit articleSuggest a changeSources

The scientist who narrates the sinking and leads the research that gave rise to SOLAR.

Contents
  • Research and responsibility
  • The reports
  • Original documents
  • Sources and credits

Research and responsibility

The Doctor attributes the attempt to concentrate too much power in a limited space to H.I.V.E.. After the sinking, he recovers the machine and establishes the laboratory that would become SOLAR.

The reports

His SOLAR Reports series follows the company’s growth, the search for the Void and the testing program. In the final volume, he takes responsibility for the decision to recruit people by force.
