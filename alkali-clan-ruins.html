Jungle ruins

Explorer Digby Braveman describes the remains of the Alkali clan in Explorer’s Log, his exploration diary. After weeks searching the dense jungle, he realizes that the largest and best-preserved ruin he has found stands near the coast. His account describes the other surviving jungle ruins as few and far between.

The stairway diary

The entries for July 14 and 15, with no year given, describe a stairway connected to a cave containing human remains, mostly skulls. Digby identifies small marks on the bones as the result of traditional Alkali body modifications. According to his examination, the skulls were forcibly separated from their bodies.

These observations lead him to ask whether the stairway once provided access to a prison. The diary leaves this interpretation as the explorer’s hypothesis. He fears that the stone-brick interior might collapse and postpones deeper excavation until he can stabilize it.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

In the July 16 entry, Digby finds a mechanical button after examining the stairway. He wonders whether the mechanism could open a secret passage and let him proceed without digging. The diary ends with this discovery, without recording the button’s activation or its result.
