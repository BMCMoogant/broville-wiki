The island mission

The monastery stands on Brolapagos, an island with a port settlement and resort in Broville’s western ocean. The community description places it on the other side of the island from the tourist area and presents it as one of the first buildings erected by settlers after their arrival.

Funerary area

One plaque commemorates Bembe Coto as the founder, while another identifies his ashes. His testament, titled My Final, lies near copies of the Old Spanish Key. The book is recorded at −2157, 72, 2372; keys are found one block below, at −2157, 71, 2372.

The testimony and the use of the key are developed in The Monastery’s Secret.

Spoiler warningReveal spoiler

In his testament, Bembe says that he is dying and regrets the destruction of the culture he found on the island. He admits that his group killed the original inhabitants while attempting to convert them and buried their bodies and cultural remains in pits beneath the mission.

Bembe says he saved something that the other monks might plunder. His clue places the entrance to the burial site at the mouth of the water and asks for a key to be returned so that he can illuminate the way. The final warning attributes a supernatural danger to the original inhabitants’ practices; this interpretation belongs to the narrator himself.
