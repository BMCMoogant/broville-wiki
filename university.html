A district between Concord Commons and Oldtown, with a campus, museum, and access to the transport network.

Campus and stations

The district occupies the area between Concord Commons and Oldtown. University Square provides a reference point for the academic area. Museum connects the purple and green lines.

Museums and archives

The Museum of Natural History holds funerary artifacts and the starting point for Chr0NOS. The New Archeology Division appears in community references to excavations.
