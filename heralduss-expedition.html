Context and where to start

King Heraldus' Journals consists of books signed “H.”, presumably King Heraldus, describing a military and mystical expedition to recover the Eye of Arburaz. Leonard, Reginald and Robert leave separate accounts that complement the king's journal.

Use the ruined castle in the swamps near the edge of the map as your reference point. Community documentation places the cavern entrance at −1414, 63, 1069. The first journal is at −1485, 27, 1131. The two Journal 6 volumes are together at −1448, 4, 1202. Item numbering and the days described in their pages are different references.

The Keeper's deadline

The first day's account, associated with King Heraldus' Journal, Part 2, has Heraldus gather Wilhelm, Leonard, Kenneth and Baldric and head for a stone structure beyond the swamp. A staircase descends to a door whose inscription tells entrants to abandon all hope. Beyond trapped corridors, they meet the elderly Keeper of the Seal. He moves a circular slab, revealing an inky black pit, announces that he will reopen it in ten days, and closes the passage behind the last knight. The expedition establishes a defensible camp and splits the search into three patrols.

Fungi, graves and losses

King Heraldus' Journal, Part 1 covers the second day and the return to camp after crossing decaying eastern caverns inhabited by creatures. Heraldus's patrol passes through a spore-filled cavern and reaches a wooded, gravestone-covered hill surrounded by water. The gravedigger directs them to seek a tall, frightening figure, but gives directions that the king considers absurd.

Leonard's Journal follows the search for a shadowy figure in the southern limestone caves, which are infested with giant spiders. Gerald is bitten and poisoned and must be carried. A dark, scaly creature emerges from the water and drags Robert into a pool; his companions cannot rescue him, and the following day's search fails.

King Heraldus' Journal, Part 3 records Kenneth's worsening condition and Baldric's good spirits despite his injuries. Reginald's Journal explains that both were too battered to continue and that Reginald remained at camp to protect them while the king went on. Kenneth dies of his injuries. The others advance through caverns, a maze and underground fortifications.

Taking the Eye

On the seventh day, a gate closes behind the knights in the throne room. Agraal, a figure in golden armor whose presence the king describes as radiating malice and murderous intent, leaps with frightening speed and strikes Wilhelm in the chest, crushing his breastplate and throwing him against a pillar. The blow kills him. The fight is described in King Heraldus' Journal, Part 4.

The group exploits moments when their opponent's weapon or gauntlet becomes lodged in the masonry. After his defeat, the body briefly rises again before being consumed by fire. Heraldus and Ulric find the Eye in the ashes. A voice introduces itself as the artifact and promises knowledge and power in return for seeing the sun again. It warns that Agraal's spirit survives and will seek another body to recover it. Heraldus accepts the bargain.

The interrupted return

Frederick carries the artifact. When their pursuers break through the lodging's door with a battering ram, three knights use the tower stairs to ambush them while Frederick and the king protect the Eye.

On the return journey, Robert reappears with scales and slits in his neck, attacks Leonard and flees. Robert's Journal records his time in the water, the hardening of his skin and the formation of slits that let him breathe underwater. Leonard recognizes his companion in the creature. King Heraldus' Journal, Part 6 II also expresses the wish to leave the following morning once the group has rested.

At camp, the survivors learn that Gerald has died of poisoning. The ninth day's journal, at −1572, 6, 1427, ends during the siege, before the scheduled reopening. It does not confirm that Heraldus escaped the caverns.

The player's encounter

Community documentation describes Agraal’s Vengeance as a wither skeleton carrying an invisible blaze, accompanied by ghasts and guards. It places the trigger at −1603, 9, 1393 and the final Eye chest at −1572, 4, 1382. These coordinates describe the map challenge; its outcome does not supply the journal's missing ending.

Another underground fraternity

Related texts mention the death of Emperor Joseph, leaving HoBro without its leader. This episode belongs to the fraternity's account and does not provide an ending for Heraldus's expedition.
